    9.3  Common Policy Mistakes and How to Fix Them
         - Mistake 1: Label selector doesn't match (check with --show-labels)
         - Mistake 2: Namespace not included (policies are namespace-scoped!)
         - Mistake 3: Forgot to allow DNS (pods need port 53 to CoreDNS!)
         - Mistake 4: Policy applied to wrong direction (ingress vs egress)
