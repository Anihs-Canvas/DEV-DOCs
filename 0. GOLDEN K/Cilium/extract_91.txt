    9.1  The Systematic Debugging Approach
         - Step 1: Is it a DNS issue? (nslookup from pod)
         - Step 2: Is it a policy issue? (cilium monitor --type policy-verdict)
         - Step 3: Is it a routing issue? (cilium endpoint inspect)
         - Step 4: Is it the application? (check app logs)
