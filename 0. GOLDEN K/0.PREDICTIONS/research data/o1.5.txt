What is Over/Under 1.5 Goals Betting?
If you are wondering how over/under 1.5 goals betting works, it's pretty simple. If you back a game to be over 1.5 goals then there must be 2 or more goals scored in total between the two teams in that match for your bet to win. If you back under 1.5 goals then there must be zero or 1 goal in total between the two teams in that match for your bet to win.
