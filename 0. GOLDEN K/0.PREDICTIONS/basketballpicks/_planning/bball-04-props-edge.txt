--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(0) TL;DR — WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The edge is LINE-SHOPPING + DE-VIG ARBITRAGE against lazy soft/DFS prop lines,
NOT model superiority. We build a "true" per-player distribution from the SHARP
consensus (Pinnacle/Circa), reprice it at each soft-book and DFS-app line, and
bet only where their number is stale enough that the true side clears their
price plus the (large) house hold. Our own model earns its keep on ONE narrow
slice — obscure lines the sharp market itself does not price (deep-bench usage,
WNBA/college, blocks/steals, post-injury minute cascades) — and NOWHERE the
sharps quote, because there their de-vig already IS the truth to within noise
(the same information ceiling that killed the soccer model).

Honest headline: ~+2% to +6% ROI per qualifying entry, HIGH variance, taxed by
fast limiting on books and capped per-entry stakes on DFS. It is a grind worth
low-four to low-five figures/yr for a disciplined solo operator, NOT a fund, and
it is SHRINKING (tools proliferating; DFS apps pivoting to peer-to-peer — see
§3d). The single biggest EV lever is not the model, it is CORRELATION on DFS
same-game stacks (§3c). Kill it fast if forward CLV ≤ 0 (§6d).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) THE EDGE, STATED PRECISELY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Three price sources for the same event "Player X points OVER/UNDER L":

  A. SHARP consensus — Pinnacle + Circa (+ sharp-ish: BetOnline, Bookmaker).
     De-vigged, this is the market's best estimate of the true probability.
     We treat it as truth. We do NOT try to beat it.
  B. SOFT sportsbooks — DK/FD/MGM/Caesars/ESPNBet. Post 200-400 lines/night,
     cannot sharpen them all, lag the sharp consensus by minutes-to-hours, and
     disagree with each other by whole points. THIS GAP is the edge.
  C. DFS pick'em — Underdog (classic vs-house, no-limit), PrizePicks/Underdog
     "Arena/Champions" (peer-to-peer), DK Pick6, Sleeper. Lines lag sharp by
     15-30 min in peak windows and sit 0.5-1.5 units off consensus; payout
     structure bakes in a large hold you must clear (§3a).

The signal, per prop, per venue:

    p_true  = de-vig(sharp two-way at sharp's line), REPRICED to the venue's
              line via an implied stat distribution   (§2)
    EDGE_book = soft_decimal_price * p_true - 1        (identical to
                sharp_anchor.edge_vs_fair — a soft book is +EV iff EDGE_book>0)
    For DFS: a leg is a candidate iff  p_true(DFS side) >= breakeven(N)+margin
             and the ENTRY EV = (Π p_true_i · corr-adj) * M(N) - 1 > 0   (§3)

Everything downstream (Kelly, CLV, kill-criteria) hangs off p_true and EDGE.
This is exactly safepicks' sharp_anchor structure, ported from 1X2/OU2.5 to
player props — with ONE new hard part props have and 1X2 did not: the venues
quote the SAME player at DIFFERENT LINES, so you cannot compare prices directly.
You must reprice. That is §2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(2) THE SIGNAL ENGINE — DE-VIG WITH A LINE SHIFT (the props-only wrinkle)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2a. SHARP "TRUE PROBABILITY" AT THE SHARP LINE  (mirror sharp_anchor.fair_*)
  For each (player, market, sharp_line) collect the two-way over/under decimals
  from the anchor set. De-vig to a fair pair {p_over, p_under}. Method choice
  matters more on props than on 1X2 because prop holds are 6-10%+:
    - Shin (safepicks default, devig.shin) — good general choice, models
      insider/favourite bias; use as primary, exactly as sharp_anchor does.
    - Multiplicative (normalize by overround) and Additive are the fast
      fallbacks; log all three and report method-sensitivity (a 2% edge that
      only survives one de-vig method is not real). Two-way markets ONLY —
      props are natively two-way, so unlike 1X2 no synthesis is needed.
  ANCHOR POLICY (mirror ADR 024): Pinnacle primary; Circa fallback; a small
  sharp-book consensus (median of de-vigged p_over) as tertiary. Record which
  anchor supplied p_true on every candidate. Where <2 sharp books quote a prop,
  it is a THIN line -> route to the model (§4), never to blind arbitrage.

2b. REPRICING TO A DIFFERENT LINE  (THE NEW PART — no 1X2 analog)
  DFS says 18.5; Pinnacle says 21.5. You cannot compare 18.5-over to 21.5-over.
  Reconstruct the player's stat distribution implied by the sharp market, then
  evaluate it at the target line:
    1. Gather ALL sharp quotes for that player+market incl. ALTERNATE lines
       (Pinnacle posts alts). De-vig each into a P(stat > line_k) point.
    2. Fit a parametric CDF to those points:
         - points / PRA / combos  -> Normal or skew-Normal (continuous-ish);
         - rebounds / assists / 3PM / blocks / steals -> Negative-Binomial or
           Poisson (low-count, right-skew — the memo's "more modelable" stats).
       One or two sharp anchor points already pin mean+dispersion; alts refine.
    3. p_true(target_line) = 1 - CDF(target_line - 0.5 continuity) for OVER.
  This step is unavoidable and it means EVEN the "pure arbitrage" path carries
  a light distributional MODEL (the CDF shape). Be honest that the edge is only
  as clean as that assumption; sanity-cap repricing to <= ~2 units from the
  nearest sharp anchor (extrapolation risk explodes past that).
  Half-point buffers matter: NBA scoring is ~continuous so a 0.5 gap is ~1-2%
  of probability; on 3PM/steals a 0.5 gap is huge — never treat integer-line
  moves as small.

2c. EDGE PER VENUE
  Soft sportsbook: EDGE_book = price * p_true - 1. Keep if >= threshold (start
  2%, sweep 2/3/4% exactly like sharp_anchor.THRESHOLDS). Line-shop across ALL
  soft books, take the max price (their market_max analog) but log single-book
  bet365-style availability too — the best-of-panel price overstates what one
  account can hold.
  DFS: see §3. The "price" is implicit in the payout multiplier and the leg
  count, so the comparison is p_true vs a BREAKEVEN, not vs a decimal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(3) THE DFS PICK'EM ANGLE (Underdog / PrizePicks / Pick6) — THE DURABLE HOME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3a. THE PAYOUT MATH AND THE HOLD YOU MUST OVERCOME
  Pick'em is a fixed-multiplier parlay. Per-leg breakeven prob b(N)=M^(-1/N)
  for all-must-hit (Power/Standard). Computed exactly:

   Entry (all-must-hit)    mult M   fair(2^N)   breakeven/leg   hold@50-50 legs
   ---------------------   ------   ---------   -------------   ---------------
   UD 2-pick                3.0x       4.0x        57.74%           25.0%
   UD 3-pick                6.0x       8.0x        55.03%           25.0%
   UD 4-pick               10.0x      16.0x        56.23%           37.5%
   UD 5-pick               20.0x      32.0x        54.93%           37.5%
   PP 6-pick flex          25.0x      64.0x        58.48%*          60.9%
   (PrizePicks Power Plays ~ same family; 6-flex pays partial, *effective be
    higher and hold ~61% — the worst product on the board.)

  READ THIS: if you throw RANDOM legs at the posted line, the house keeps
  25-61% of your money. The hold is enormous vs a sportsbook's ~4.5-10%. The
  ONLY reason DFS is +EV is that a stale DFS line at 18.5 (vs sharp 21.5) can
  push p_true(over) to 62-70%, far above the 55-58% breakeven. You are not
  beating a small vig; you are exploiting a WHOLE-POINT line error big enough
  to swamp a big vig. No line error -> no edge -> you are donating.
  Structural note: 3-pick (55.03%) and 5-pick (54.93%) have the LOWEST
  breakevens; 2-pick (57.74%) and 4-pick (56.23%) are structurally more
  expensive. Prefer odd-leg power plays, all else equal.

3b. PER-LEG EDGE -> ENTRY EV
  Leg qualifies iff  p_true(DFS side) >= b(N) + margin (start margin = 0.03).
  Entry EV (power/all-must-hit):  EV = P_joint * M(N) - 1, where P_joint is the
  correlation-adjusted joint hit prob (§3c). Flex/insured entries pay on 1-2
  misses; their payout is a random variable, so EV = Σ_k P(k of N hit)·pay(k)-1
  and you MUST compute the full distribution (do not eyeball flex EV).

3c. CORRELATION — THE SINGLE BIGGEST EV LEVER (and the noisiest, most policed)
  DFS prices legs as INDEPENDENT (multiplier = independent-fair minus hold). Any
  real POSITIVE correlation among your legs raises P(all hit) above the product
  of marginals -> pure EV the book did not charge for. Bernoulli joint:
     P(both) = p1·p2 + rho·sqrt(p1(1-p1)·p2(1-p2))
  Worked 2-leg power play (M=3), both legs p_true=0.58:
     rho=0.00 -> P=0.336, EV = +0.9%   (barely alive on pure line edge)
     rho=0.10 -> P=0.361, EV = +8.2%
     rho=0.20 -> P=0.385, EV = +15.5%
  At p=0.60: rho 0.00/0.10/0.20 -> EV +8.0% / +15.2% / +22.4%.
  So a modest positive correlation is worth MORE than the line edge itself.
  Sources of real positive correlation to STACK (same direction):
     - Game pace/total up -> multiple players' counting-stat OVERS together
       (both teams' scorers' points; a PG's assists + teammates' points).
     - A blowout risk correlates star UNDERS (minutes capped) — stack unders.
     - Within-player: points-over and PRA-over on the same player (near-
       deterministic positive corr — but books often BLOCK same-player combos).
  BRUTAL CAVEATS: (i) rho is estimated from thin, noisy data — a wrong +0.2
  assumption manufactures phantom EV; be conservative, cap the credited rho at
  ~0.15 unless you have a fitted game-level joint model. (ii) DFS apps
  increasingly RESTRICT or re-price obvious same-game/same-player stacks and
  demote correlated slips. (iii) Negative correlation (two players competing
  for the same rebounds/usage) SILENTLY KILLS all-must-hit slips — screen for
  it and never co-rostered anti-correlated overs. Correlation is the edge's
  turbo AND its biggest self-deception risk.

3d. *** THE 2026 STRUCTURAL SHIFT — PEER-TO-PEER (updates the feasibility memo) ***
  Since the memo, regulatory pressure (cease-and-desist in NY, FL, MI, MA, MD,
  OH, CT, IA, TN and more) pushed the vs-the-house model out of many states.
  PrizePicks transitioned ALL contests to its peer-to-peer "Arena" format
  nationwide; Underdog runs "Champions" P2P in banned states. THIS MATTERS:
    - vs-HOUSE pick'em (classic): you beat a STALE HOUSE LINE. The devig+
      dispersion edge above applies directly. Underdog's classic vs-house
      product (legal states, advertises NO limiting) is the PRIME venue.
    - PEER-TO-POOL (Arena/Champions): you no longer beat a house line — you beat
      the FIELD of other entrants, minus rake (~the same hold, taken as rake).
      Devig still tells you which side is +EV, but your realized edge is now
      (your edge) minus (the sharpest other players' edge) minus rake. As sharp
      money and OddsJam/Outlier users crowd the same +EV legs, P2P edge
      COMPRESSES toward the rake. This is the existential trend risk to the
      whole thesis: the durable, no-limit venue is exactly the one being
      converted to a format where the edge decays as the field sharpens.
  IMPLICATION FOR SCOPE: prioritise (1) Underdog classic vs-house where it still
  exists; (2) obscure legs (WNBA/college/deep bench) where the FIELD is soft
  even in P2P; and treat P2P NBA-star props as near-dead (sharp field).

3e. STAKE SIZING — QUARTER-KELLY ON THE ENTRY (mirror quarter_kelly_fraction)
  The BET is the entry, not the leg. Kelly for a multiplier bet paying (M-1)
  to 1 at win prob P_joint:  f* = (P_joint·M - 1)/(M - 1); then f = 0.25·f*,
  capped at max_stake_pct_bankroll (seed 0.02), floored at 0 — identical shape
  to valuebets.quarter_kelly_fraction, returning every intermediate for audit.
  PORTFOLIO CAP: entries on a slate SHARE LEGS -> bankroll outcomes are
  correlated; a naive per-entry Kelly over-bets the slate. Cap AGGREGATE nightly
  exposure (e.g. <= 5-10% bankroll across all live entries) and de-duplicate
  overlapping legs, exactly as the sharp_anchor report warns that one match
  contributing several correlated selections narrows the true CI.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(4) WHERE OUR MODEL ACTUALLY ADDS VALUE (honest: a thin slice, and never vs sharps)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ~90% of realized edge is pure devig+line-shop. Zero model needed; OddsJam /
  Unabated / Outlier already sell this and are faster than a home scraper.

  The model helps in EXACTLY ONE regime: props the SHARP market prices thinly or
  not at all, so there is no de-vig truth to copy and the DFS/soft line is the
  ONLY number:
    - WNBA / NCAA small-conference / EuroLeague obscure props (thin sharp books).
    - NBA neglected stats: blocks, steals, deep-bench minutes/usage, second-unit
      scoring — books lavish attention on points, neglect these.
    - INJURY-CASCADE window: star ruled out -> main line reprices in minutes (no
      edge), but the usage/minutes cascade onto 5-8 teammates' props reprices
      slowly and unevenly. A minutes->usage->NB model can price the cascade
      before the soft/DFS line catches up. This is the model's best moment.
  Model spec (v1): minutes projection (rotation + injury news) -> usage share ->
  per-stat Negative-Binomial/Poisson rate -> P(stat > line). Same nba_api-fed
  pattern the feasibility memo scoped.

  HARD HONESTY BOUND (the soccer ceiling, restated): on ANY prop Pinnacle/Circa/
  DK/FD price with real volume, their de-vig IS p_true to within noise. Your
  model CANNOT beat it — the residual is information they have and you don't
  (final minutes intent, load management, sharp flow). So the model is a
  FALLBACK ANCHOR for uncovered lines, never a primary signal, and every model-
  sourced candidate must be flagged "model-anchored, no sharp cross-check" and
  held to a HIGHER edge threshold + tighter stake, because it has no sharp
  price validating it. If the model ever appears to "beat" a well-quoted sharp
  line, assume a bug or a stale pull before assuming alpha.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(5) THE LIMITING PROBLEM + HONEST LIFETIME-VALUE CEILING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SPORTSBOOKS (DK/FD/MGM/Caesars): the edge is a WASTING ASSET per account.
  ML risk systems flag persistent +EV-vs-sharp betting fast (winners cut to
  $75, or new prop accounts capped at ~$10, within days-to-weeks — worse on
  props post-Jontay-Porter, where structural prop limits and under-bet bans
  were added). Mitigations are palliative, not cures:
    - Bet EARLY (soft lines are stalest right after posting), round stakes to
      look recreational, don't only bet the steam side, spread across books,
      mix in main-market noise. Extends life from days to weeks, not to years.
    - Realistic per-book LTV: extract a few thousand dollars, get throttled,
      done. Books are a SMASH-AND-GRAB layer, not the business.

  DFS pick'em: the durable-but-capped home.
    - Underdog classic advertises NO limiting -> the one place you can persist.
      BUT: per-entry stakes are capped (often $100-$250, less on obscure props),
      daily volume is bounded by how many qualifying legs exist (~5-30/night in
      season), and PrizePicks silently demotes to flex-only after ~200 winning
      entries. So even "no limit" is capped by stake ceilings and leg supply.
    - P2P conversion (§3d) caps it further by decaying the edge toward rake as
      the field sharpens.

  HONEST LTV CEILING (solo operator, few-thousand-$ bankroll, disciplined):
    - Per-night realized EV in season: single-digit to low-double-digit dollars
      early, scaling with bankroll and leg supply, minus brutal variance.
    - Annual take: LOW FOUR to LOW FIVE FIGURES, and DECLINING year over year as
      tools proliferate, sharp books reprice props in minutes, and DFS goes P2P.
    - This is NOT a scalable quant fund. It is a part-time edge with a shelf
      life. Size ambition — and engineering spend — accordingly. If the plan
      needs >$300/mo of tooling to run, the tooling can eat the whole edge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(6) FORWARD VALIDATION HARNESS  (mirror paper_clv.py + a weekly beat)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Prop odds history only reaches ~2023 and is thin -> a Last-Dance-grade
  backtest is UNDERPOWERED. Validate FORWARD, paper first. Reuse paper_clv's
  read-only, empty-runs-clean, formula-shared discipline verbatim.

6a. TWO CLV MEASURES (props need both — the sharp truth AND the venue number)
  When a candidate is FLAGGED, snapshot: DFS/soft line + our p_true + the sharp
  consensus de-vigged p_sharp(our side). At LOCK (tip-off) snapshot the sharp
  CLOSING consensus and the venue's CLOSING line. Then:
    CLV_sharp = p_sharp_close(our side) / p_sharp_flag(our side) - 1
        -> did the SHARP market drift toward our side? Positive = we were on the
           right side of information (the real, honest signal — same role as
           paper_clv's O_early/O_close, inverted to probability space).
    CLV_venue = p_venue_implied_close(our side) / p_venue_implied_flag - 1
        -> did the SOFT/DFS number itself move to our side (i.e., did we beat
           the number they eventually posted)? Positive = we captured a stale
           line before it corrected.
  A REAL edge shows CLV_sharp > 0 (we predate information) OR CLV_venue > 0 with
  CLV_sharp ~ 0 (we harvested a genuine stale-line dispersion). If BOTH are ~0
  and realized ROI is positive, it is variance, not edge — do not scale.
  Settlement: grade actual stat vs venue line -> W/L/push -> realized ROI-at-
  entry, reusing an engine.pnl_per_unit-style settler (half/push handling like
  paper_clv._early_pnl). Paper-trade ALL of this before a cent is real.

6b. PRE-REGISTERED THRESHOLDS (a props mini-G1 — write them down, then don't move)
    - min per-leg edge margin over breakeven: 0.03 (sweep 0.02/0.03/0.04).
    - min de-vig agreement: >= 2 sharp books, and edge must survive Shin AND
      multiplicative de-vig (method-robust, per §2a).
    - max repricing distance from nearest sharp anchor: 2.0 units.
    - correlation credited: capped at rho <= 0.15 without a fitted joint model.
    - min forward sample before real money: >= 500 graded paper legs OR 8 weeks,
      whichever is later; per-venue and per-league sub-samples reported (WNBA,
      NBA-neglected, NCAA separately — pooled hides where the edge really is).
    - model-anchored (no sharp cross-check) candidates: 2x the edge threshold,
      half the stake, tracked as a SEPARATE cohort.

6c. THE WEEKLY BEAT (ops cadence, mirrors safepicks JobRun discipline)
    A scheduled job that, each week: pulls the week's flagged->locked->settled
    paper picks; recomputes CLV_sharp, CLV_venue, realized ROI with bootstrap
    95% CI (seeded, like bootstrap_yield_ci); breaks down by venue/league/stat/
    de-vig method; writes an evidence-only report (no auto-enable, no bet
    writes) + a JobRun audit row. Green light to risk real money requires a
    human read of that report clearing 6b — never an automatic promotion.

6d. THE KILL CRITERION (pre-committed — decide now, execute without flinching)
  Declare the operation DEAD and stop if ANY of:
    (K1) After the 500-leg/8-week paper window, mean CLV_sharp 95% CI <= 0
         (no informational edge) AND CLV_venue 95% CI <= 0 (no stale-line
         capture). Positive realized ROI without positive CLV = luck; kill.
    (K2) Realized paper ROI 95% CI <= 0 after crediting only rho<=0.15 and at
         ACHIEVABLE per-entry stakes (not the best-of-panel fantasy price).
    (K3) Qualifying-leg supply < ~5/night sustained -> not worth the time even
         if per-leg edge is real (the soccer "volume-starved" failure mode).
    (K4) LIVE, post-launch: within 8 weeks of real money the prime venue
         (Underdog classic) is limited/demoted, or the P2P field compresses
         edge to < time cost, such that per-entry EV < the value of the hour
         spent. Smash-and-grab the books, then stop.
  Any single K firing = stop. Do NOT lower thresholds to keep it alive — that is
  the exact self-deception (chasing a dead pocket) the soccer verdict warned of.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(7) EXPECTED ROI, VARIANCE, AND WHAT "SUCCESS" LOOKS LIKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  EXPECTED ROI (per qualifying entry, disciplined, forward-validated):
    - Single +EV legs at soft books: ~+2% to +4% ROI, before limiting tax.
    - DFS power plays on line-edge alone (no correlation): +0% to +3% — the
      hold barely cleared; margin for error is thin (see §3a: p=0.58 -> +0.9%).
    - DFS with genuine, conservatively-estimated positive correlation: +5% to
      +12% on 2-3 leg stacks — but this is the number most inflated by bad rho
      estimates, so trust it LEAST and paper it HARDEST.
    - Model-anchored obscure/WNBA/cascade legs: potentially higher (thin market)
      but unvalidated and highest-variance; treat as speculative until 6b clears.

  VARIANCE: severe and non-negotiable. A 3-leg power play hits ~18-22% of the
  time; a +6% EV entry still LOSES ~80% of the time. You need HUNDREDS of entries
  to distinguish edge from noise — which is also long enough for limiting/
  demotion to bite. Bankroll must survive 15-20 consecutive entry losses (real
  at these hit rates); quarter-Kelly + the aggregate slate cap (§3e) is the
  floor of discipline, not a nicety.

  WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE (set expectations honestly): a small, real, positive-
  CLV grind that nets low-four-to-low-five figures a year at low limits with high
  variance and a declining trend line — a proof that "beat the bookies" is
  literally true in this one derivative corner, not a business that scales. If
  the goal is a scalable product, this is not it (and neither was soccer). If the
  goal is "extract real money from bookmaker/DFS laziness before it closes," this
  is the single best vehicle examined — run it lean, validate forward, kill on
  schedule.

(8) DATA + BUILD PLAN (reuse the Django/Postgres + safepicks odds architecture)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DATA:
    - nba_api (free): box/PBP/advanced/lineups/aggregated tracking -> minutes/
      usage/pace model + settlement of realized stats. Injury feeds: ESPN/
      rotowire (mandated, cleaner than soccer's).
    - The Odds API BUSINESS ($99/mo): REQUIRED for player props + Pinnacle +
      historical (~2023). Free/Pro tiers have NO props — do not bother.
    - DFS lines: Underdog/PrizePicks lines via a real-time feed (Outlier/OddsJam/
      Unabated) or a home poller; a vendor +EV feed is faster than DIY and may
      be worth it ONLY if its cost < measured edge (it often isn't — watch K-
      criteria on tooling spend).
    - HONEST DATA GAP: raw optical tracking is paywalled; you get aggregates
      (fine for props). Prop history ~2023 is too thin for a rigorous backtest
      -> forward paper only, per §6.

  COMPONENTS (each a near-direct port of a safepicks file — DO NOT modify
  safepicks; build fresh in basketballpicks mirroring the pattern):
    - devig/true-line builder + line-shift repricer  <- mirrors sharp_anchor.py
      (fair_* / edge_vs_fair) + adds the §2b CDF repricing (the new piece).
    - DFS pick'em EV + breakeven + correlation-adjusted joint  <- new, §3.
    - quarter-Kelly-on-entry stake + slate portfolio cap  <- mirrors
      valuebets.services.quarter_kelly_fraction (audit-dict shape identical).
    - forward paper-CLV harness (two CLV measures, empty-runs-clean, read-only)
      <- mirrors paper_clv.py; add the weekly-beat JobRun report (evidence-only,
      no auto-enable) mirroring sharp_anchor's build_report/JobRun discipline.
    - minutes->usage->NB prop model (fallback anchor for thin lines only) <- new.
  TIMELINE: 4-8 week MVP, ~$100-300/mo running. Engineering risk is LOW; the
  real risk is that the only live edge gets limited / goes P2P before it pays
  back the build. Gate the build on the paper window clearing §6b.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(9) ONE-SCREEN SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * EDGE = de-vig the sharp consensus into a true distribution, reprice it at
    each soft/DFS line, bet where their stale line beats it past their (large)
    hold. Pure arbitrage; not forecasting.
  * MODEL matters ONLY on lines the sharps don't price (WNBA/college/blocks/
    deep-bench/injury-cascade) — a thin, higher-threshold, half-stake cohort.
    Never vs a well-quoted sharp line (soccer ceiling holds).
  * BIGGEST LEVER is not the model — it's conservative positive CORRELATION on
    DFS same-game stacks (a +0.9% line-only entry becomes +15% at rho=0.2), also
    the noisiest and most policed input.
  * DURABLE HOME = Underdog classic vs-house (no limit); books are smash-and-
    grab (limited in days-weeks); DFS is capped by stakes/leg-supply and is
    going peer-to-peer, which decays the edge toward rake.
  * VALIDATE FORWARD: two CLV measures (sharp-drift + venue-drift), 500-leg/
    8-week paper mini-G1, weekly evidence-only beat, pre-registered thresholds.
  * EXPECT ~+2-6% ROI/entry, HIGH variance, low-4-to-low-5 figures/yr solo,
    declining. KILL on: CLV<=0, ROI CI<=0 at real stakes, leg-supply<5/night, or
    venue limiting/P2P-compression. Do not lower thresholds to keep it alive.
