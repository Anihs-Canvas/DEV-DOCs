--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE REFRAME THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING (read first)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The paid design (bball-05) is built around one load-bearing assumption:
"Pinnacle (eu) is the primary sharp anchor; Business tier buys it." THAT
ASSUMPTION IS ALREADY DEAD, PAID OR NOT.

  *** Pinnacle shut down public API access on 2025-07-23. *** It now serves only
  "select high-value bettors & commercial partnerships." The Odds API LOST
  Pinnacle as a source and publicly replaced it with Betfair Exchange, SingBet,
  and Polymarket. So the $99/mo Business tier would NOT deliver a Pinnacle prop
  anchor today either. [VERIFIED — odds-api.io + The Odds API blog, 2025-26]

Consequence: the marginal cost of going $0 is NOT "you lose Pinnacle." Nobody
below a commercial-partner tier has Pinnacle props anymore. The real question
collapses to: **can a free SOFT-BOOK CONSENSUS (built from DK/FD/MGM/Caesars/
Bovada public JSON) devig cleanly enough to beat stale DFS lines — the same
consensus-fallback anchor bball-05 already specified in §2d, now PROMOTED from
fallback to primary?** That is the hypothesis this memo pressure-tests (§3).

One genuinely free trickle of Pinnacle STILL exists (OddsPapi resells it, 250
full-boards/mo free — §1.D1), enough for a low-n sharp CROSS-CHECK but nowhere
near enough to anchor a full slate. We exploit exactly that: soft-consensus for
volume, OddsPapi-Pinnacle for an ~8-games/mo honesty audit.

The devig raw material. Need TWO-SIDED over/under per (player, market, line) so
Shin devig can run.

A1. SPORTSGAMEODDS — FREE "Amateur" tier          [DOC, strongest free structured]
    Provides: NBA + WNBA (8 leagues) player props + team markets from 9 books
      (FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars, ESPN BET, BOVADA, Unibet,
      PointsBet, William Hill), as clean structured JSON. Crucially ships a
      "Fair Odds" and "Book Consensus" field ON THE FREE TIER — a pre-computed
      devig/consensus you would otherwise build by hand.
    Live/historical: live.
    Limits: 10 req/min, **2,500 objects/month** (the binding constraint — an
      "object" ≈ one market/odds record, so a full prop board burns objects
      fast; budget it like credits).
    Free-forever: yes, no card. No Pinnacle, no PrizePicks/Underdog on free.
    ToS/brittleness: sanctioned API, low brittleness — the cleanest free feed
      here. Object cap is the ceiling, not IP bans.
    Role: PRIMARY structured soft-book prop + team feed AND a ready-made
      consensus/fair-odds field for the anchor (§1.D2). Single best free find.

A2. THE ODDS API — FREE "Starter" tier            [LIVE-HIT 401 (key required)]
    Provides: NBA/WNBA/NCAAB player props (player_points, player_rebounds,
      player_assists, player_threes, ..._alternate, ..._q1) for US soft books,
      + team markets. NO Pinnacle (dropped after the 2025 shutdown; Betfair
      Exchange/SingBet/Polymarket added instead).
    Live/historical: live only (historical is paid, 10x multiplier).
    Limits: **500 credits/mo.** events + sports endpoints are FREE (enumerate
      game IDs at zero cost). Bulk team call = markets×regions (h2h,spreads,
      totals × us = 3 credits). Per-event props = unique-markets-returned ×
      regions. Math: 500 credits ≈ 83 team calls/mo (~2-3/day) OR ~80-100
      single-event prop pulls/mo. A TRICKLE — one marquee game's props/day, or
      daily team markets, not both, not a full slate.
    Free-forever: yes. ToS: sanctioned, robust. Role: secondary/spot cross-book
      prop + reliable team markets when SGO objects run low.

A3. BOVADA public JSON (offshore)                 [LIVE-HIT — reachable, no geoblock!]
    Provides: NBA (offseason→empty in July) + WNBA (LIVE — I pulled 3 games:
      spread/ML/total JSON). Bovada DOES carry two-sided player props + alts

    Endpoint (team, verified live): GET https://www.bovada.lv/services/sports/

    Limits: none published; poll politely (1 req / 5-10 s). Free-forever
      (unofficial). ToS/brittleness: reverse-engineered, US-legal-grey (offshore)

      backbone of the soft panel (works where DK/FD scrapers get blocked).

A4. DRAFTKINGS / FANDUEL / BETMGM public JSON      [GATED — 403 to datacenter IP]
    Provides: the deepest US soft prop boards (200-400 lines/night). DK path

=======================  (D) THE DEVIG ANCHOR — the hardest gap  ============
D1. ODDSPAPI — FREE tier, the only free Pinnacle left  [DOC — verify on first key]
    Provides: 250 requests/mo where EACH request returns the ENTIRE board for one
      fixture — every one of ~370 bookmakers, every market, "no multiplier" —
      INCLUDING Pinnacle + Betfair Exchange, with player props (playerName field)
      and HISTORICAL odds free at every tier (no 10x penalty).
    Endpoint shape: GET /v4/odds?fixtureId={id}&bookmakers=pinnacle,fanduel,...
      (+ /tournaments, /odds-by-tournaments for enumeration).
    Limits: **250 req/mo ≈ 8 full boards/mo** — a TRICKLE. How they still have
      Pinnacle post-shutdown is unstated (commercial partner or site-scrape) →
      LONGEVITY + ToS RISK; treat as could-vanish. Pinnacle's PROP menu is thin
      anyway (it prices few props), so some pulls return Pinnacle team-only.
    Role: **NOT a volume anchor.** Use the 250 pulls surgically — at CLOSING, on
      your highest-conviction flagged games — to compute a low-n but REAL
      CLV_sharp / sharp-beat audit (§4b-3 of bball-05) that soft-consensus alone
      cannot give. It is the single thread of independent sharp truth in a $0
      world. [Independent verification REQUIRED before relying on it.]

D2. SOFT-BOOK CONSENSUS pseudo-book  (the workhorse anchor — free, built in-house)
    The bball-05 §2d "market_consensus" fallback, PROMOTED to primary. For each
    (player, market, line): Shin-devig every non-DFS book quoting it (SGO panel +
    Bovada + Odds-API + any DK/FD when a residential poller exists), take the
    sharp-weighted MEDIAN fair prob → p_fair. Line-normalize across half-points
    (bball-05 §2b CDF repricing) before comparing to a DFS line.
    SGO's built-in "Fair Odds"/"Book Consensus" field (A1) is a zero-effort first
    cut of exactly this — but recompute it yourself so you control the devig
    method + can log method-sensitivity (a 2% edge surviving only one devig
    method is not real).
    HONESTY TAG on every record: anchor="pinnacle" (rare, OddsPapi) vs
    anchor="consensus" (usual) — split coverage per anchor so a consensus-only
    stretch is read as COVERAGE, not efficiency (the bball-05 trap).

D3. NOT viable free for props: Betfair Exchange (team only, no US props),
    odds-api.io free (2 books, no consensus field), any "Pinnacle alternative"
    vendor (all paid). Circa/BetOnline/Bookmaker have no free public JSON feed.

N/A — nba_api / hoopR / wehoop / euroleague-api: rich box/PBP/minutes/usage, ZERO
    odds. Correctly scoped in the plan as SETTLEMENT + model-input only, never a
    price source. Free-forever, no key (nba_api), keyless. Keep as-is.

3. THE KEY HYPOTHESIS, PRESSURE-TESTED
   "Can free-scraped SPORTSBOOK-consensus props be a sharp-enough devig anchor to
    beat free DFS lines — i.e. carry the whole edge at $0?"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VERDICT: PARTIALLY YES — real on the liquid core, degrades to NOISE on the
thin/model-anchored corner. Precisely:

WHY IT CAN WORK (the honest positive case):
  * You do NOT need Pinnacle-grade sharpness to beat DFS. You only need your
    anchor to be SHARPER THAN THE DFS LINE — and DFS pick'em lines are
    structurally staler and softer than DK/FD/MGM (they update slower, shade for
    the house/rake, and lag the injury cascade by 15-30 min). For mainline props
    on rotation players (PTS/PRA/REB/AST), 5+ soft books converge to within ~1-2%
    of each other; that tight consensus IS within noise of true, and the stale
    DFS number sitting 0.5-1.5 pts off it is a real, capturable dispersion edge.
  * Pinnacle's disappearance is INDUSTRY-WIDE (§0): OddsJam/Outlier/Unabated are
    ALSO devigging soft consensus now. You are not structurally disadvantaged vs
    the paid tools on the anchor — only on speed and coverage.

WHY IT DEGRADES (the honest negative case — do not wave this away):
  * A consensus of SOFT books is not an independent sharp signal; it is the
    average opinion of the same lazy market you are partly betting into. When all
    the soft books are wrong in the SAME direction (correlated error — the
    classic pre-injury-news or public-team bias), your "truth" is wrong with them,
    and DFS may be wrong the same way. You lose the ability to distinguish "I beat
    true probability" from "I and the softs share a blind spot." Pinnacle's whole
    value was being the OUTSIDE price-maker that exposed exactly that shared error.
  * You LOSE the gold-standard honesty metric. bball-05 §4b-3 / bball-04 §6a made
    SHARP-BEAT CLV (taken price vs the SHARP's own closing fair) the verdict
    metric. At $0 that metric can only be computed on the ~8 OddsPapi-Pinnacle
    games/mo — orders of magnitude below the n≥500 the gate needs. The gate MUST
    be rewritten (§4) to lean on CLV_VENUE (did the DFS/soft number itself move to
    me) + realized ROI, with the Pinnacle pulls as a low-n bias spot-check.
  * On the THIN corner the plan reserves for the model — WNBA obscure, NCAA
    small-conf, blocks/steals, deep-bench, injury-cascade legs — there is NO soft
    consensus (books disagree wildly or don't price it) AND no sharp cross-check.
    At $99/mo you had a thin Pinnacle line to sanity-check the model there; at $0
    you have NOTHING validating it. That cohort goes from "half-stake speculative"
    to "display-only / unbettable" — it is pure model-vs-itself, the exact soccer
    failure mode (confidence that correlated NEGATIVELY with CLV).

BOTTOM LINE: the free soft-consensus anchor carries the LIQUID-MAINLINE-vs-DFS
edge (the bulk of realistic $0 volume) but cannot carry the thin/model corner and
cannot give you the sharpest honesty metric. The edge survives at maybe ~60-75%
of the paid design's strength on the part that was ever realistically bettable
solo — and 0% on the corner that needed the sharp line. Net: a real but
NARROWER edge, and a WEAKER lie-detector guarding it.

4. THE $0 INGESTION DESIGN (drops into bball-05 in place of the Business tier)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Keep the ENTIRE bball-05 architecture (PropSnapshot model, BudgetedClient,
per-game closing capture, prop_clv harness, pre-registered thresholds, settlement
via nba_api). Only the SOURCES + the ANCHOR + the GATE METRIC change.

4a. SOURCE STACK (multi-tenant free budgets, each its own BudgetedClient):
    - SGO client   : 2,500 objects/mo budget. Priority-reserve floors as
                     bball-05 (CRITICAL final-window pulls protected to the last
                     object; LOW open sweeps sacrificed first). Primary props+team.
    - OddsAPI client: 500 credits/mo. events FREE → enumerate IDs; spend credits
                     on team markets daily + Betfair team anchor + a spot prop
                     pull only on the day's marquee game.
    - Bovada client: no formal cap, slow polite bucket, browser UA. Datacenter-
                     safe → the always-on backbone of the soft panel.
    - OddsPapi client: 250 req/mo budget, spent ONLY at closing on flagged
                     marquee games (the sharp audit sample). Never on open sweeps.
    - DFS clients (Underdog, PrizePicks): own slow buckets, browser UA + Referer,
                     TTL cache, generous safety-ceiling "budget"; no-op skip_job
                     on 403 (no key/endpoint → no crash, per bball-05 §2f).
    - Optional residential DK/FD poller: enrich-only, never load-bearing.

4b. THE ANCHOR (replaces "Pinnacle primary / consensus fallback"):
    PRIMARY  = soft-book CONSENSUS pseudo-book (§1.D2): sharp-weighted median of
               Shin-devigged fair probs across SGO panel + Bovada + Odds-API
               (+DK/FD when available). Line-normalized via the §2b CDF reprice.
    AUDIT    = OddsPapi Pinnacle closing pulls on flagged games (low-n CLV_sharp).
    Every record tagged anchor="consensus"|"pinnacle"; coverage split per anchor.

4c. THE GATE METRIC (rewritten for $0 — the one real design change to bball-05):
    OLD verdict metric: SHARP-BEAT CLV vs Pinnacle close, n≥500. UNCOMPUTABLE now.
    NEW primary metric: CLV_VENUE — signed move of the DFS/soft line itself toward
      our pick between flag and lock (bball-05 §4b-2). A real stale-line edge
      shows the NUMBER consistently coming to us. Pair with realized ROI-at-flag
      and a GAME-BLOCK bootstrap CI (not i.i.d. — one game's legs are correlated).
    SECONDARY: the OddsPapi CLV_sharp on the ~8/mo audit games — reported as a
      bias check, NOT as the gate (n too small to gate on).
    Re-frozen pre-registered thresholds (write once, move only via dated ADR):
      CONFIRM cell: n≥500 settled legs AND mean CLV_venue ≥ +0.5 line-pts (or the
        prob-equivalent) AND game-block CI lower bound > 0 AND realized-ROI CI
        lower bound > -0.5% AND sustained ≥8 weeks AND the OddsPapi audit does NOT
        show consensus systematically biased vs Pinnacle on the same legs.
      KILL cell: n≥300 AND (CLV_venue CI upper bound < 0 OR realized-ROI CI upper
        bound < 0).  HOLD otherwise. All cells DISABLED at birth.

4d. CADENCE (bball-05 §5 beats, re-sourced — unchanged shape):
    open sweep (SGO+Bovada, LOW) → hourly intraday (NORMAL) → 10-min final window
    inside T-90m (CRITICAL, objects/credits reserved here) → per-game closing flag
    (DB-only) + OddsPapi Pinnacle pull on flagged marquee closings → nba_api box
    settle → weekly prop_clv on CLV_venue → monthly cell verdict.

5. HONEST DEGRADATION + KILL CRITERION for the $0 path
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW MUCH THE EDGE DEGRADES vs the (already-Pinnacle-less) paid design:
  * Liquid-mainline-vs-DFS edge: ~LITTLE degradation. SGO/Bovada consensus is
    close to what paid Odds API Business would give (both now Pinnacle-less), so
    on PTS/PRA/REB/AST for rotation players the $0 stack is ~85-95% as good; the
    only real loss is SPEED (soft scrapers lag a paid push feed by seconds-minutes
    in the injury-cascade window — where the edge is fattest, so this hurts more
    than 5-15% sounds).
  * Thin/model-anchored corner (WNBA obscure / NCAA / blocks-steals / deep-bench /
    cascade): ~FULL degradation to noise. No consensus, no sharp cross-check →
    DISPLAY-ONLY. This is a real capability loss, but bball-04 §4 already rated it
    speculative + highest-variance, so we lose a cohort that was never bankable.
  * Honesty harness: MODERATE degradation — verdict metric downgraded from
    independent-sharp CLV to venue-drift CLV + a low-n Pinnacle audit. Weaker
    lie-detector; the game-block bootstrap + the OddsPapi bias check are the
    partial compensations.

KILL-CRITERION (pre-committed; fires in ADDITION to bball-04 §6d K1-K4):
  K5 ($0-specific): if, after the 500-leg / 8-week paper window, CLV_VENUE 95% CI
     ≤ 0 on the LIQUID cohort (the only cohort a $0 stack can bank) → the soft
     consensus is not sharper than the DFS line → the whole $0 thesis is dead;
     keep the honest predictor, drop the betting claim. Do NOT rescue it by
     lowering thresholds or by pretending the thin corner works.
  K6 (anchor-integrity): if the OddsPapi Pinnacle audit shows soft-consensus
     SYSTEMATICALLY biased (not merely noisy) vs Pinnacle on the same legs — i.e.
     our "truth" is contaminated by shared soft-book error — kill immediately;
     a biased anchor manufactures phantom edge (the deepest $0 hazard).
  K7 (supply/feed-starvation): if Cloudflare/geo blocking on the DFS + DK/FD
     scrapers sustains qualifying-leg supply < ~5/night, the pipeline is
     effectively dead regardless of theoretical edge — same as bball-04 K3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE SINGLE BIGGEST RISK OF GOING $0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT the money and NOT the scraper fragility (real but engineerable). It is the
ANCHOR-CONTAMINATION risk: with no independent sharp price, your "true"
probability is a devig of the same soft market you're grading against, so the
honesty gate — the ONE discipline that separates this project from the soccer
self-deception — can confirm a PHANTOM edge when the soft books and the DFS line
are wrong together. The 250-req/mo OddsPapi Pinnacle audit (K6) is the only thin
guardrail against it, and it may not survive Pinnacle's ongoing lockdown. If that
guardrail vanishes, run the predictor and DO NOT bet.
