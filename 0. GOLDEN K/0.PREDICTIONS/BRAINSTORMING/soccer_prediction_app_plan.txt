0. THE PRODUCT — A SAFE-PICKS ENGINE (THE SAFETY DOCTRINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MISSION: every day, surface a SHORT list (0-5) of the highest-probability
  selections in world soccer — hit-rate target 88%+ — each published with
  the exact minimum odds that make it worth taking, staked flat, tracked
  honestly. Everything else the platform can do is scaffolding around
  that list.

  0.1 What "safest market" buys you — and what it does not
    * "Safe" = per-bet certainty (high hit rate). It does NOT automatically
      mean profit or a safe bankroll. At short odds the margin for error
      is razor thin, because of the BREAK-EVEN WALL:

        odds taken   break-even hit rate     (BE = 1/odds)
        ----------   -------------------
          1.10             90.9%
          1.15             87.0%
          1.20             83.3%
          1.25             80.0%
          1.30             76.9%
          1.35             74.1%
          1.40             71.4%
          1.50             66.7%

    * Consequence: a genuinely excellent 90%-accurate feed is PROFITABLE
      at average odds 1.18 (ROI = 0.90 x 1.18 - 1 = +6.2% per bet) and a
      SLOW LOSER at average odds 1.10 (ROI = -1.0%). The product's edge
      is therefore not just picking winners — it is refusing every price
      below the floor. Price discipline IS the product.

    * THE SLOW-BLEED TRAP (the #1 killer of "safe tips" products): a feed
      can win 88% of bets for months while losing money invisibly,
      because each win returns pennies and each loss eats many wins.
      Defenses are engineered in: the price floor (0.2), the de-vig edge
      gate (§8.1), and the slow-bleed detector in the backtester and the
      live monitor (§9).

  0.2 The price floor (published with every pick)
      Every pick carries its minimum acceptable odds:
        min_odds = (1 + m) / p_cal
      where p_cal is the calibrated probability and m the required margin
      (default m = 3%). Example: p_cal = 0.90 -> min_odds = 1.144.
      The pick is published as "TAKE ONLY AT >= 1.15". If the market never
      offers the floor, the pick silently expires — that is the system
      working, not failing.

  0.3 The safe-market basket (full ranking + per-market spec in [ODDS §2])
      S1  Double Chance 1X — dominant home favorite    p ~0.88-0.95
      S2  Draw No Bet      — same fixture profile      p ~0.84-0.92 (+void)
      S3  Over 1.5 goals   — scoring leagues only      p ~0.78-0.86
      S4  Asian Handicap -1.0 — heavy favorites        win ~0.55-0.65 + push
      S5  Asian Handicap +1.5 — underdog, balanced games  cover ~0.72-0.80
      S6  Under 3.5 goals — chronic unders leagues only   p ~0.78-0.84
      (S5/S6 added v3.1 as CANDIDATES — enabled only via G1 evidence.)
      EXCLUDED from the core despite high hit rates: Over 0.5 goals and
      anything priced 1.01-1.09 — the break-even wall makes them
      mathematically unworkable at retail prices. Candidate markets are
      screened by the five tests in [ODDS §2.5].

  0.4 Product hierarchy (this ordering governs every section below)
      CORE       : the daily SAFE PICKS list (S1-S4 cells only)
      SUPPORTING : probability engine, odds engine, backtester, monitors —
                   they exist to make the core list trustworthy
      SECONDARY  : the VALUE tier (+EV mid-odds hunting), exotic markets,
                   deep-learning experiments — OFF by default, Phase 6,
                   never allowed to dilute the core list (§8.8)

  0.5-legacy note: high accuracy is NOT the same claim as near-100%.
      The theoretical 1X2 ceiling is ~75-80%; bookmakers run ~55-58%.
      The safe basket reaches 85-93% only because it bets DERIVED markets
      that bundle outcomes (1X covers two of three results). The three
      pillars still stand under everything: CALIBRATION, VALUE (price
      floor), RISK CONTROL.

0.5 PREDICTABILITY vs BEATABILITY — TWO DIFFERENT TARGETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Field observation: lower leagues often FEEL more predictable than top
  leagues. Decompose carefully — two different things get called
  "predictable" and they point in opposite directions:

  A) RAW OUTCOME PREDICTABILITY (model accuracy / log-loss):
     Top leagues usually WIN here. Lower divisions have more parity,
     higher draw rates, lower scoring, heavier churn, more motivation
     noise — models show WORSE accuracy and calibration there.

  B) MARKET BEATABILITY (edge vs the bookmaker):
     Lower leagues usually WIN here, and this is what the feeling really
     is. The EPL closing line is the sharpest market in sports — disagree
     with it and you are usually wrong. A lower-league line is set by a
     junior model with fat margins — disagree with it and you are right
     far more often. The predictability did not go up; the competition
     got worse.

  MEASUREMENT — Market Beatability Index (MBI), per league and per
  (league, market) cell, computed by the backtester (§9):
      (a) model log-loss                    — raw predictability
      (b) de-vigged closing-line log-loss   — market sharpness
      MBI = (b) - (a)                        — the gap = harvestable edge

  SAFE-FIRST APPLICATION: the MBI is computed for the SAFE CELLS first
  (S1-S4 x whitelisted leagues). A safe cell is enabled only when its MBI
  is positive on an adequate sample — i.e. our probabilities for that
  cell are demonstrably sharper than the prices we would take.

  CAUTIONS (why "bet the lowest leagues" is still wrong): thin data =>
  miscalibrated confidence; integrity risk concentrates low (fixing can
  fake short "predictable" streaks); books cap stakes low and limit
  winners fast. Sweet spot: mid-tier European leagues [ODDS §5].

  In prose: a single Django project owns eleven apps, one per concern.
  The safepicks app is the product; the valuebets app is the demoted
  secondary module (identical machinery, different thresholds, disabled
  until Phase 6). Everything persists to one PostgreSQL instance; Celery
  Beat drives all recurring jobs; model training happens offline and
  publishes versioned artifacts the service layer loads for inference.

  Key principles (unchanged from v2.0):
   * PostgreSQL is the single source of truth — everything versioned.
   * Celery Beat drives all recurring work (no cron sprawl).
   * Training OFFLINE, inference ONLINE. Never train in a request.
   * Every prediction row stores model version, feature snapshot hash,
     probability vector, and the odds available at prediction time.

3. DATA SOURCES (THE MOAT — BUDGET TIERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Model quality is capped by data quality. Plan for 3 tiers:

  TIER 0 — FREE (MVP, historical bootstrap):
    * football-data.co.uk        — 25+ seasons CSV, results + closing odds
                                   for ~25 leagues. THE backtesting
                                   goldmine. League codes in [ODDS §5].
    * football-data.org API      — fixtures, results, standings (free).
    * Understat / FBref scraping — xG, shot data (respect robots.txt/ToS).
    * OpenLigaDB, API mirrors    — supplemental.

  TIER 1 — CHEAP PAID (production MVP):
    * API-Football (api-sports.io) — fixtures, LINEUPS, INJURIES, live
      scores, odds from 20+ books, ~$25-60/mo tiers.
    * The Odds API — clean multi-bookmaker odds feed for line shopping
      and closing-line capture.

  TIER 2 — SERIOUS EDGE (later):
    * StatsBomb / Opta / Sportmonks — event-level data. Only when ROI
      justifies.
    * Pinnacle API (via broker) — sharpest closing lines = calibration
      benchmark.

  SAFE-FIRST NOTE: lineup and injury feeds are promoted in importance.
  The #1 source of "safe pick" upsets is late team news in mismatch
  fixtures (rotation before cup/European games, rested stars). The
  lineup-confirmed re-check (§5.4, §8.1) depends on Tier 1 data —
  budget for it from Phase 3, not later.

  Ingestion rules (unchanged): raw JSONB + parsed rows, immutable raw;
  canonical IDs + TeamAlias + fuzzy matcher + review queue on day one;
  idempotent syncs keyed on (source, external_id).

  Data-completeness scoring: every match scored [0,1] at feature-build
  time (history depth, odds coverage, xG/lineup/injury availability).
  Stored on the feature snapshot; the safe engine refuses to publish
  below threshold — STRICTER for the core product than for the
  secondary module. A safe pick with unverifiable team news is not safe.

  API budget & rate-limit rules: per-source daily budget, token-bucket
  limiter, backoff + jitter, Redis cache with per-endpoint TTL, job_run
  audit rows. Final-2h polling protected first when budget runs low.

5. FEATURE ENGINEERING (THE REAL DIFFERENTIATOR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  All features computed "as of" kickoff-minus-X, never after. Feature
  sets versioned. Unchanged from v2.0 except priorities:

  5.1 Strength ratings — Elo (home-adv term, goal-diff multiplier, K per
      league), Dixon-Coles attack/defense with time decay, optional
      Glicko-2 / pi-ratings.
  5.2 Form & rolling windows — 5/10/20-match EW: PPG, GF/GA, xG for/
      against, shots (on target), corners, clean-sheet rate, BTTS rate,
      O2.5 rate, first-half goal rate; home/away splits; rest days,
      congestion, travel (Phase 3), competition context.
  5.3 Head-to-head — last-N with decay; style-matchup proxies.
  5.4 Squad signals (PROMOTED — Phase 3 for the core, was "Phase 2+"):
      starting-XI strength delta vs season average (lineups ~1h before
      kickoff -> the LINEUP-CONFIRMED RE-CHECK that guards every safe
      pick, §8.1); key-player availability index (minutes- and
      goals-weighted, from official injury/suspension feeds); keeper
      change flag; accumulated-cards suspension tracker (a predictable
      absence the market sometimes misses in smaller leagues).
  5.5 Market features — opening/current implied probs (de-vig), line
      velocity, sharp-vs-soft divergence. Train market-free AND
      market-aware variants; disagreement = candidate edge.
  5.6 Context — position, motivation proxies, season stage, derby flag,
      weather (Phase 3), referee tendencies (secondary markets only),
      manager change recency. Dead-rubber and rotation-risk flags are
      PROMOTED: they gate safe picks (§8.1).
  5.7 Data-quality features — completeness score in the vector AND as
      the publish gate; league format flags (playoff splits).

  5.8 Transfer, news & player-performance signals (v3.2)
    TRANSFERS (M6 — API-Football transfers endpoints, season-boundary
    and window snapshots):
    * Squad-churn indices: share of last season's minutes lost/gained
      in the window; starters-lost-to-bigger-leagues flag.
    * Key-player departure flag: any top-3 by minutes or goals leaves
      -> widen rating uncertainty for ~6-10 rounds (exactly when
      early-season models are blindest; pairs with time-decayed Elo).
    * Integration lag: new signings' share of the projected XI — high
      churn + high new-signing share = calibration-risk flag that can
      HOLD a safe pick even when p_cal clears the gate.
    NEWS (policy, not scraping ambition):
    * STRUCTURED news only as gates: official injury/suspension lists,
      confirmed lineups, manager changes (§5.6). These are facts.
    * UNSTRUCTURED news (press conferences, beat reporters, rotation
      rumours) = Phase 6 NLP research behind a flag; noisy, laggy and
      manipulable — it may inform a HOLD, never a publish. No pick is
      ever published on news sentiment.
    PLAYER PERFORMANCE (Phase 6 — Tier 2 event data):
    * Availability-weighted attack/defense strength from player
      ratings, xG-chain involvement, progressive actions; keeper
      shot-stopping over/under-performance (PSxG-GA).
    * Only when Tier 2 data cost is justified by a proven core
      [PLAN §3]; per-league cost/benefit via its own mini-gate.
    HONESTY: stacked, 5.4 + 5.8 buy ~1-3% log-loss vs B0, steadier
    band-ECE, and roughly 88 -> 90-92% hit rate at unchanged
    selectivity. The irreducible remainder (red cards, deflections,
    keeper errors) caps every model well short of 100% — the min_prob
    dial, not features, is the raw hit-rate lever [ODDS §3].

  De-vig: multiplicative for MVP -> Shin's method (favourite-longshot
  aware — matters MORE at odds-on prices, exactly our range).

6. MODELING STRATEGY (CALIBRATION-FIRST, SAFE MARKETS FIRST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.1 Baselines (build FIRST)
    B0: Market baseline — de-vigged closing odds AS the prediction.
        The honesty benchmark; every safe cell must beat it (MBI > 0).
    B1: Poisson GLM — scoreline matrix -> every derived market's
        probability (1X, DNB, O1.5, AH-1.0) from ONE model.
    B2: Dixon-Coles — B1 + low-score correction + time decay. The
        low-score correction matters MORE for us than for 1X2 bettors:
        O1.5 and DC probabilities live exactly in the 0-0/1-0/0-1 cells
        it corrects. Validate against published results.

  6.2 ML layer — per-safe-market binary GBMs are the PRIMARY models
    M1: LightGBM binary "does 1X cover?" per fixture profile.
    M2: LightGBM binary Over 1.5; separate model per league cluster.
    M3: LightGBM binary "favorite covers AH -1.0".
    M4: (secondary module) multiclass 1X2 + O2.5/BTTS models — the old
        VALUE-tier models, Phase 6.
    Monotonic constraints on rating-diff features throughout.

  6.3 Ensembling & calibration — WHERE THE SAFE PRODUCT LIVES OR DIES
    * Stack: logistic meta-learner over {B2-derived prob, M1/M2/M3,
      market prob}.
    * Calibrate every output; then VERIFY calibration specifically in
      the operating band [0.80, 0.97] with high-resolution reliability
      bins: "BAND-ECE". A model can look calibrated overall and be 3
      points optimistic at 0.90 — which at odds 1.18 is the difference
      between +6% and roughly break-even. Band-ECE < 0.02 is the gate.
    * Metrics that matter, in order (v3.0 re-ranked):
        1. Band-ECE on [0.80, 0.97] + reliability diagrams
        2. Log-loss / Brier vs B0 per SAFE CELL (=> cell MBI)
        3. Backtested ROI & max drawdown of the safe list at flat stakes
        4. CLV of published picks vs closing
      (Accuracy reported, never used for selection.)

  6.4 Training protocol (unchanged, leakage paranoia): walk-forward
      time splits only; per-league models where data allows, pooled with
      league embeddings otherwise; weekly retrain + daily rating updates;
      Optuna budgeted on walk-forward CV; MLflow everything.

  6.5 Deep learning — SECONDARY MODULE, Phase 6+, only after the safe
      engine is profitable. Expected +1-2% log-loss at 10x complexity.

  6.6 The unit of edge is the (league, market) SAFE CELL
    * S1-S6 x whitelisted leagues = the candidate cell grid (S5/S6
      added v3.1). Every cell benchmarks
      against B0 -> cell MBI; cells enabled in market_rule by backtest
      evidence only (positive MBI AND positive flat-stake yield AND
      band-ECE < 0.02 on >= 2 seasons).
    * Expect the grid to be SPARSE: maybe 10-25 enabled cells worldwide.
      That is enough. A short trustworthy list beats a long noisy one.
    * Draws get no cell, ever, in the core. (Secondary module, Phase 6+.)

7. ODDS ENGINE, PRICE FLOORS & "BEST ODDS" SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Polling: every 6h at T-7d, hourly at T-24h, every 5-10 min final 2h.
  * Line shopping: best decimal odds per (match, market, selection)
    across books — worth 1-3% ROI, and for odds-on betting it is often
    THE difference between clearing the price floor and not.
  * Closing-line capture (is_closing=TRUE) for every market — CLV + MBI.
  * De-vig every book (Shin) -> consensus prob weighted toward sharps.
  * Steam / sharp-divergence detection — alerts + features.

  Price-floor mechanics (v3.0 — the core publishing rule):
    * Every candidate pick computes min_acceptable_odds = (1+m)/p_cal
      (m per market_rule, default 3% core / 1.5% for S1 at p>=0.93).
    * Published picks display the floor. The engine re-checks the best
      available price at publish time and every poll; if the market
      never meets the floor before kickoff -> status=EXPIRED, no bet.
      EXPIRED is a healthy outcome and is reported as such.
    * Timing: odds-on favorites usually shorten as kickoff approaches
      (public money piles on) -> capture EARLY (T-3d to T-24h) is the
      default for S1/S2/S4; O1.5 prices are stabler. Backtest per cell
      and store the optimal capture window on market_rule.
    * Routing: soft books for price capture (stale prices = floor met
      more often), sharps/exchanges for volume and account longevity
      (limits_winners flag).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE SAFE PICK ENGINE (CORE PRODUCT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8.1 Publish gates — a pick appears ONLY when ALL pass:
      1. Cell enabled: (league, market) is an enabled safe cell (§6.6)
      2. Probability: p_cal >= market_rule.min_prob
         (S1: 0.88 / S2: 0.85 / S3: 0.80 / S4: 0.72 eff. — tuned by
          backtest, seeds in [ODDS §3])
      3. Band-calibration current: production model's live band-ECE
         within 2x of backtest value
      4. Price: best available odds >= min_acceptable_odds (floor, §7)
         AND within the cell's odds window AND <= 7.0 global ceiling
      5. Edge: de-vigged edge >= cell threshold (1.5-3%) — yes, even
         safe picks must beat the de-vigged market or they slow-bleed
      6. Data: completeness score >= safe threshold (stricter than
         secondary); league LPI >= 60; integrity blacklist clean
      7. Context: no dead-rubber flag, no rotation-risk flag UNLESS the
         lineup-confirmed re-check (below) clears it
      8. LINEUP-CONFIRMED RE-CHECK (Phase 3+): ~60-75 min before kickoff
         re-run inference with confirmed XIs; if p_cal drops below the
         gate, retract the pick (status change + notification). Late
         team news is the #1 safe-pick killer; this is the seatbelt.

  8.2 Staking — FLAT, not Kelly (v3.0)
      * At odds-on prices Kelly is misleading: p=0.90 @ 1.20 gives
        f* = 0.40 -> quarter-Kelly 10% of bankroll on ONE pick. The 2%
        cap would always bind, so the cap IS the policy. Simplify:
        FLAT 1.0-1.5% of bankroll per pick (risk_profile.staking_mode =
        FLAT, flat_stake_pct = 0.015 default).
      * Portfolio caps: max 5 open picks; max 4% on one match; max 15%
        of daily staked volume on one league; same-match picks = one
        exposure.
      * LOSING-STREAK MATH (plan for it, don't be surprised by it):
        at p = 0.88, expected LONGEST losing streak ~ ln(N)/ln(1/0.12):
        ~3 over 300 picks, ~4 over 1500 picks. At flat 1.5% that is a
        4.5-6% drawdown from streaks alone — normal operation, printed
        on the dashboard so nobody panics into changing the system.
      * Circuit breakers: pause publishing if rolling-50-pick hit rate
        drops below the cell-weighted break-even + 1%, or rolling CLV
        < 0 over 30 picks, or drawdown > 10%, or live band-ECE > 2x
        backtest. All parameters in risk_profile, editable in admin.

  8.3 Accumulator policy — SINGLES ONLY (v3.0)
      * Safe picks invite acca temptation ("three 90% picks = easy
        1.7x!"). Reality: hit rate compounds down (0.9^3 = 73%) and any
        leg below its price floor drags the whole ticket -EV. The core
        product publishes singles only.
      * A "double builder" MAY ship in the secondary module later, but
        only combining picks that INDIVIDUALLY passed every gate, with
        variance warnings, and never in the core list.

  8.4 Pick lifecycle
      generated -> gates (8.1) -> published with price floor ->
      re-checked each poll + lineup re-check -> taken/EXPIRED ->
      settled -> P&L, CLV, hit rate -> daily + weekly reports.

  8.5 Thin-week rule: the default answer in a thin week is "fewer
      picks", never "lower thresholds". ZERO PICKS IS A CORRECT OUTPUT
      and the UI treats it as a first-class state, not an error.

  8.6 Transparency (product feature, not compliance): every pick links
      to its full audit trail — probability, floor, edge, completeness,
      cell history, and after settling, its contribution to the rolling
      hit rate. Trust in a safe-picks product IS the hit-rate ledger.

  8.8 SECONDARY MODULES (everything that used to be co-primary)
      * VALUE tier (+EV mid-odds hunting, 1.60-2.60 band): the complete
        v2.0 value engine remains implemented in valuebets/ but ships
        DISABLED. Enable in Phase 6 for users who opt in. Its picks are
        listed on a separate page, never mixed into the safe list.
      * Exotic markets (BTTS, O2.5, corners/cards), draw specialist,
        deep learning, in-play: Phase 6+ experiments behind flags.
      * Secondary modules may not consume the core's API budget or
        delay its jobs (Celery queues separated: `core` vs `secondary`).

9. BACKTESTING FRAMEWORK (BUILD BEFORE BELIEVING ANYTHING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Vectorized pandas engine over historical matches + odds
    (football-data.co.uk closing odds sufficient for MVP).
  * Walk-forward: train < S, predict season S, run the FULL gate
    pipeline (8.1) + flat staking, settle, compound. Never random folds.
  * Honesty rules: odds available at prediction time (or closing as
    conservative proxy) minus 1-2% slippage; void/postponed handling;
    as-of assertions on every feature.
  * Outputs: equity curve, ROI/pick, hit rate per cell, EXPIRED rate,
    drawdown, CLV distribution, band-ECE reliability plots, bootstrap
    p-value of profit vs luck.

  SLOW-BLEED DETECTOR (v3.0 — the safe product's own alarm):
    * For every rolling 200-pick window compute ROI and its bootstrap
      95% CI. Alarm when the CI upper bound < +0.5%: the feed may be
      hitting 88% and still bleeding — this catches it in ~2 months
      instead of a year. Runs in backtest AND live (§10.2 daily job).
    * Also decompose bleed source when alarmed: hit-rate shortfall vs
      price shortfall (avg odds taken below floor+margin) vs cell mix.

  Per-league/cell decomposition report (writes league_metric):
    (a) model log-loss, (b) closing-line log-loss, (c) MBI = (b)-(a),
    plus hit rate, avg odds, yield per cell. Ranked by MBI this table
    IS the strategy; it flips market_rule cells on/off quarterly.

  Promotion rule: a model/config ships ONLY if it beats the champion on
  band-ECE AND cell MBI AND flat-stake yield over >= 2 full seasons
  across >= 3 leagues.

12. HONEST SUCCESS CRITERIA (WHAT "BEST" LOOKS LIKE — SAFE-FIRST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Product: >= 88% hit rate on the published safe list over 300+
    settled picks; EXPIRED rate 10-30% (proves the floor is doing its
    job); zero picks published that failed any gate (audited).
  * Calibration: live band-ECE < 0.02 on [0.80, 0.97]; live-vs-backtest
    ratio < 2x at all times.
  * Economics: +2-5% ROI per settled pick at flat stakes; slow-bleed
    detector green (rolling-200 ROI CI excludes <= +0.5%); max drawdown
    < 10%; positive CLV on >= 55% of taken picks.
  * League strategy: every enabled cell justified by positive MBI +
    positive yield + band-ECE < 0.02 over >= 2 seasons; the /leagues
    page can explain WHY any cell is on or off.
  * Engineering: full audit trail pick -> raw data/odds/features/model;
    zero-leakage verified; retrain-to-production one reviewed click.
  * Discipline: the system publishes NOTHING on most thin days, retracts
    picks on bad lineups, and lets floors expire unmet. That restraint
    is the product.

13. FAILURE MODES & ANTI-PATTERNS (HOW THESE SYSTEMS ACTUALLY DIE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. DATA LEAKAGE — post-kickoff data in features: spectacular backtests,
     ruinous live. Defense: frozen snapshots, as-of test assertions.
  2. BACKTEST OVERFITTING — thresholds tuned till history looks great.
     Defense: walk-forward only, >=2 seasons x >=3 leagues, bootstrap
     p-values, holdout seasons.
  3. ODDS AVAILABILITY BIAS — backtesting prices you could not have
     taken. Defense: slippage haircut, closing-as-proxy, paper-trade
     comparison.
  4. IGNORING NEGATIVE CLV — bankroll up on luck while CLV is negative.
     Defense: CLV circuit breaker trumps the equity curve.
  5. CHASING THE TAIL — "8% edge" on a 9.0 longshot = tail
     miscalibration. Defense: 7.0 ceiling + calibration-range gate.
  6. QUOTA PRESSURE — publishing because users expect picks today.
     Defense: thin-week rule; zero picks is a designed state.
  7. SOFT-DATA MIRAGE — "predictable" low league that is thin data or a
     fixed match. Defense: completeness gate, integrity blacklist, MBI
     on adequate samples only.
  8. STAKING DRIFT — overriding stakes after wins/losses. Defense:
     computed flat stakes, logged; manual bets flagged separately.
  9. SILENT MODEL ROT — league regime changes outdate the model.
     Defense: quarterly LPI/MBI review, live band-ECE monitor,
     champion/challenger.
 10. INFRA DEBT AT THE WORST TIME — odds polling dies on matchday.
     Defense: job_run audits, Sentry alerts on missed beats,
     publish-time re-checks.
 11. SLOW BLEED (v3.0 — THE safe-product killer) — 88% hit rate, money
     quietly gone: average odds taken crept below the floor+margin, or
     calibration slipped 2 points at 0.90. Defense: price floors on
     every pick, band-ECE gate, slow-bleed detector with bootstrap CI,
     bleed-source decomposition.
 12. ACCUMULATOR TEMPTATION (v3.0) — combining "sure things" until the
     ticket is -EV and high-variance. Defense: singles-only core (§8.3);
     the UI never offers combination in the core list.

14. GLOSSARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1X2            Match result market: Home (1), Draw (X), Away (2).
  Acca           Accumulator/parlay — multiplied-odds multi-leg bet.
                 Core product: singles only (§8.3).
  AH             Asian Handicap; AH -1.0 on heavy favorites = safe cell
                 S4 (push insurance at -1.0 exactly).
  Band-ECE       Expected Calibration Error measured ONLY inside the
                 operating band [0.80, 0.97] — the safe product's core
                 model metric (§6.3).
  Break-even     1/odds — the hit rate below which a price loses money.
  BTTS           Both Teams To Score (secondary module market).
  CLV            Closing Line Value — % by which taken odds beat the
                 close. Best long-run profit predictor.
  DC             Double Chance (1X / X2) — safe cell S1.
  De-vig         Removing bookmaker margin to recover true implied
                 probabilities (Shin's method).
  DNB            Draw No Bet — stake refunded on draw; safe cell S2.
  Edge           p_model_calibrated - p_market_devig.
  EV             p x (odds-1) - (1-p) per unit staked.
  EXPIRED        Pick whose price floor was never met before kickoff —
                 a healthy outcome, tracked as a product metric.
  Flat staking   Fixed % of bankroll per pick (core default 1.5%) —
                 replaces Kelly at odds-on prices where caps always bind.
  LPI            League Predictability Index (0-100, [ODDS §5]);
                 >= 60 = whitelisted.
  MBI            Market Beatability Index = closing-line log-loss minus
                 model log-loss per (league, market) cell; positive =
                 harvestable edge (§0.5).
  O/U            Over/Under total goals; O1.5 = safe cell S3.
  Price floor    min_acceptable_odds = (1+m)/p_cal — published with
                 every pick; below it, no bet (§0.2, §7).
  Slow bleed     Losing money at a high hit rate via bad prices or
                 small calibration drift — failure mode #11.
  Steam move     Sharp-book line move from informed money.
  Vig/Margin     Bookmaker profit built into prices.
  xG             Expected Goals — shot-quality scoring model.
