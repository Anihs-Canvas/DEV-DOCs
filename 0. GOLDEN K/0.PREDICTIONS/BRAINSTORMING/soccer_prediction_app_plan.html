The Product — a Safe-Picks Engine REWRITTEN v3.0

MISSION: every day, surface a SHORT list (0–5) of the highest-probability selections in world soccer — hit-rate target 88%+ — each published with the exact minimum odds that make it worth taking, staked flat, tracked honestly. Everything else is scaffolding around that list.

🧱0.1 · The break-even wall

"Safe" = per-bet certainty (high hit rate). It does not automatically mean profit — at short odds the margin for error is razor thin:

Odds takenBreak-even hit rate (1/odds)
1.1090.9%
1.1587.0%
1.2083.3%
1.2580.0%
1.3076.9%
1.4071.4%

A genuinely excellent 90%-accurate feed is profitable at avg odds 1.18 (ROI = 0.90 × 1.18 − 1 = +6.2%/bet) and a slow loser at 1.10 (−1.0%). The edge is not just picking winners — it is refusing every price below the floor. Price discipline IS the product.

🩸The slow-bleed trap

The #1 killer of "safe tips" products: a feed can win 88% of bets for months while losing money invisibly — each win returns pennies, each loss eats many wins. Engineered defenses:

  • The price floor on every pick (0.2)
  • The de-vig edge gate — even safe picks must beat the market (§8.1)
  • The slow-bleed detector in backtest and live (§9)

📏0.2 · The price floor — published with every pick

min_odds = (1 + m) / p_cal p_cal = calibrated probability · m = required margin (default 3%). Example: p_cal 0.90 → floor 1.144 → pick published as "TAKE ONLY AT ≥ 1.15".

If the market never offers the floor, the pick silently EXPIRES — that is the system working, not failing (healthy EXPIRED rate: 10–30%).

0.5

Predictability vs Beatability

Lower leagues often feel more predictable than top leagues. Two different things get called "predictable", and they point in opposite directions.

🔬A · Raw Outcome Predictability top leagues win

Model accuracy / log-loss. Lower divisions have more parity, higher draw rates, lower scoring, heavier churn, more motivation noise — models show worse accuracy and calibration there.

🥊B · Market Beatability low leagues win

Edge vs the bookmaker — what the feeling really is. The EPL closing line is the sharpest market in sports; a lower-league line is set by a junior model with fat margins. The predictability did not go up; the competition got worse.

📐The Market Beatability Index (MBI)

(a) model log-loss  ·  (b) de-vigged closing-line log-loss  ·  MBI = (b) − (a) (a) = raw predictability · (b) = market sharpness · MBI = the gap = harvestable edge

Safe-first application: the MBI is computed for the SAFE CELLS first (S1–S4 × whitelisted leagues). A safe cell is enabled only when its MBI is positive on an adequate sample — i.e. our probabilities for that cell are demonstrably sharper than the prices we would take.

Cautions — why "bet the lowest leagues" is still wrong: thin data ⇒ miscalibrated confidence; integrity risk concentrates low (fixing can fake short "predictable" streaks); books cap stakes and limit winners fast. Sweet spot: mid-tier European leagues [ODDS §5].

Data Sources — The Moat

Model quality is capped by data quality. Three budget tiers.

TIER 0 · Free MVP bootstrap

  • football-data.co.uk — 25+ seasons CSV, results + closing odds, ~25 leagues. THE backtesting goldmine. Codes in [ODDS §5].
  • football-data.org API — fixtures, results, standings.
  • Understat / FBref — xG, shot data (respect ToS).
  • OpenLigaDB + mirrors — supplemental.

TIER 1 · Cheap Paid production MVP

  • API-Football — fixtures, LINEUPS, INJURIES, live scores, odds from 20+ books, ~$25–60/mo.
  • The Odds API — multi-bookmaker odds feed for line shopping + closing capture.

TIER 2 · Serious Edge later

  • StatsBomb / Opta / Sportmonks — event-level data, when ROI justifies.
  • Pinnacle API — sharpest closing lines = calibration benchmark.
Safe-first note: lineup and injury feeds are PROMOTED in importance. The #1 source of safe-pick upsets is late team news in mismatch fixtures (rotation before cup/European games, rested stars). The lineup-confirmed re-check (§5.4, §8.1) depends on Tier 1 data — budget for it from Phase 3, not later.

📥Ingestion rules

  • Raw JSONB + parsed rows; raw is immutable, parsers re-runnable.
  • Canonical IDs + TeamAlias + fuzzy matcher + review queue on day one.
  • Idempotent syncs keyed on (source, external_id).

🧪Data-completeness scoring

Every match scored [0,1] at feature-build time. Stored on the snapshot; the safe engine refuses to publish below threshold — stricter for the core than for the secondary module. A safe pick with unverifiable team news is not safe.

🚦API budget & rate limits

Per-source daily budget, token-bucket limiter, backoff + jitter, Redis cache per-endpoint TTL, job_run audit rows. Final-2h polling protected first when budget runs low.

Feature Engineering — The Real Differentiator

All features computed "as of" kickoff-minus-X, never after. Versioned feature sets. Unchanged content from v2.0 — with two promotions for the safe product.

💪5.1 · Strength ratings

Elo (home-adv term, goal-diff multiplier, K per league); Dixon-Coles attack/defense with time decay; optional Glicko-2 / pi-ratings.

📈5.2 · Form & rolling windows

5/10/20-match EW: PPG, GF/GA, xG, shots (on target), corners, clean-sheet/BTTS/O2.5/first-half-goal rates; home/away splits; rest days, congestion, travel (Phase 3), competition context.

🤝5.3 · Head-to-head

Last-N with decay; style-matchup proxies (possession-vs-counter from shot/possession stats).

🧑‍🤝‍🧑5.4 · Squad signals PROMOTED — Phase 3 core

Starting-XI strength delta vs season average (lineups ~1h pre-kickoff → the lineup-confirmed re-check that guards every safe pick, §8.1); key-player availability index (minutes- and goals-weighted, from official injury/suspension feeds); keeper change flag; accumulated-cards suspension tracker.

💹5.5 · Market features

Opening/current implied probs (de-vig), line velocity, sharp-vs-soft divergence. Train market-free AND market-aware variants; disagreement = candidate edge.

🌍5.6 · Context flags PROMOTED

Position, motivation proxies, season stage, derby flag, weather (Phase 3), referee tendencies (secondary markets only), manager change recency. Dead-rubber and rotation-risk flags now gate safe picks (§8.1).

🧪5.7 · Data-quality features

Completeness score in the vector AND as the publish gate; league format flags (playoff splits AUT/BEL).

➗De-vig method

Multiplicative for MVP → Shin's method (favourite-longshot aware — matters MORE at odds-on prices, exactly our range). Library: shin (PyPI).

5.8 · Transfer, News & Player-Performance Signals NEW v3.2

🔁Transfers M6 · API-Football

  • Squad-churn indices — share of last season's minutes lost/gained in the window; starters-lost-to-bigger-leagues flag.
  • Key-player departure flag — a top-3 by minutes/goals leaving widens rating uncertainty for ~6–10 rounds, exactly when early-season models are blindest.
  • Integration lag — high churn + high new-signing share of the projected XI = calibration-risk flag that can HOLD a safe pick even when p_cal clears the gate.

📰News policy gates, not sentiment

  • Structured news only as gates: official injury/suspension lists, confirmed lineups, manager changes. These are facts.
  • Unstructured news (pressers, beat reporters, rotation rumours) = Phase 6 NLP research behind a flag — noisy, laggy, manipulable. It may inform a HOLD, never a publish.

⭐Player performance Phase 6 · Tier 2 data

  • Availability-weighted attack/defense strength from player ratings, xG-chain involvement, progressive actions; keeper PSxG−GA over/under-performance.
  • Only when Tier 2 data cost is justified by a proven core; per-league cost/benefit via its own mini-gate.
Honesty: stacked, §5.4 + §5.8 buy ~1–3% log-loss vs the market baseline, steadier band-ECE, and roughly 88 → 90–92% hit rate at unchanged selectivity. The irreducible remainder (red cards, deflections, keeper errors) caps every model well short of 100% — the min_prob dial, not features, is the raw hit-rate lever [ODDS §3].
06

Modeling Strategy — Calibration-First, Safe Markets First

6.1 · Baselines — build FIRST

B0 Market baseline

De-vigged closing odds AS the prediction. The honesty benchmark; every safe cell must beat it (MBI > 0).

B1 Poisson GLM

Scoreline matrix → every derived market's probability (1X, DNB, O1.5, AH−1.0) from ONE model.

B2 Dixon-Coles

B1 + low-score correction + time decay. The low-score correction matters MORE for us: O1.5 and DC probabilities live exactly in the 0-0/1-0/0-1 cells it corrects.

6.2 · ML layer — per-safe-market binary GBMs are the PRIMARY models

M1 "Does 1X cover?"

LightGBM binary per fixture profile — the S1 flagship model.

M2 Over 1.5

LightGBM binary; separate model per league cluster (S3).

M3 "Favorite covers AH −1.0"

LightGBM binary with push-aware target encoding (S4).

M4 Secondary module

Multiclass 1X2 + O2.5/BTTS models — the old VALUE-tier models, Phase 6.

🎯6.3 · Calibration — where the safe product lives or dies v3.0

  • Stack: logistic meta-learner over {B2-derived prob, M1/M2/M3, market prob}; calibrate every output (isotonic / beta).
  • Then verify calibration specifically in the operating band [0.80, 0.97] with high-resolution reliability bins: "BAND-ECE". A model can look calibrated overall and be 3 points optimistic at 0.90 — at odds 1.18 that is the difference between +6% and break-even. Band-ECE < 0.02 is the gate.

Metrics that matter, in order (v3.0 re-ranked):

  1. Band-ECE on [0.80, 0.97] + reliability diagrams
  2. Log-loss / Brier vs B0 per SAFE CELL (⇒ cell MBI)
  3. Backtested ROI & drawdown of the safe list at flat stakes
  4. CLV of published picks vs closing

🧯6.4 · Training protocol + 6.5 · Deep learning

  • Walk-forward time splits only — never random K-fold.
  • Per-league models where data allows; pooled + league embeddings otherwise.
  • Weekly retrain; daily rating updates; Optuna budgeted on walk-forward CV; MLflow everything.
  • Deep learning: secondary module, Phase 6+, only after the safe engine is profitable (+1–2% log-loss at 10× complexity).

🧩6.6 · The unit of edge is the (league, market) SAFE CELL

  • S1–S6 × whitelisted leagues = the candidate cell grid (S5/S6 added v3.1). Every cell benchmarks against B0 → cell MBI; cells enabled in market_rule only by backtest evidence: positive MBI AND positive flat-stake yield AND band-ECE < 0.02 over ≥ 2 seasons.
  • Expect the grid to be SPARSE: maybe 10–25 enabled cells worldwide. That is enough. A short trustworthy list beats a long noisy one.
  • Draws get no cell, ever, in the core (secondary module, Phase 6+).
07

Odds Engine, Price Floors & "Best Odds" Selection

📡Core engine

  • Polling: every 6h at T-7d, hourly at T-24h, every 5–10 min final 2h.
  • Line shopping: best price per selection across books — worth 1–3% ROI, and at odds-on it is often THE difference between clearing the price floor and not.
  • Closing-line capture for every market — CLV + MBI.
  • De-vig every book (Shin) → consensus prob weighted toward sharps.
  • Steam / divergence detection — alerts + features.

📏Price-floor mechanics v3.0 — the core publishing rule

  • Every candidate pick computes min_acceptable_odds = (1+m)/p_cal (m per market_rule; default 3%, 1.5% for S1 at p ≥ 0.93).
  • Published picks display the floor; the engine re-checks the best price at publish time and every poll. Floor never met before kickoff → status=EXPIRED, no bet. EXPIRED is a healthy outcome and is reported as such.
  • Timing: odds-on favorites usually shorten toward kickoff (public money) → capture EARLY (T-3d to T-24h) for S1/S2/S4; O1.5 prices are stabler. Backtest per cell; store the window on market_rule.
  • Routing: soft books for price capture (stale prices = floors met more often); sharps/exchanges for volume and account longevity.
08

The Safe Pick Engine CORE PRODUCT

🚧8.1 · Publish gates — a pick appears ONLY when ALL pass

  • Cell enabled: (league, market) is an enabled safe cell (§6.6)
  • Probability: p_cal ≥ market_rule.min_prob (S1: 0.88 / S2: 0.85 / S3: 0.80 / S4: 0.72 eff. — tuned by backtest, seeds in [ODDS §3])
  • Band-calibration current: live band-ECE within 2× of backtest value
  • Price: best odds ≥ the price floor AND within the cell's window AND ≤ 7.0 global ceiling
  • Edge: de-vigged edge ≥ cell threshold (1.5–3%) — yes, even safe picks must beat the de-vigged market or they slow-bleed
  • Data: completeness ≥ safe threshold (stricter than secondary); LPI ≥ 60; integrity clean
  • Context: no dead-rubber flag, no rotation-risk flag unless the lineup re-check clears it
  • LINEUP-CONFIRMED RE-CHECK (Phase 3+): ~60–75 min before kickoff, re-run inference with confirmed XIs; if p_cal drops below the gate, retract the pick (status change + notification). Late team news is the #1 safe-pick killer; this is the seatbelt.

⚖️8.2 · Staking — FLAT, not Kelly v3.0

  • At odds-on prices Kelly is misleading: p=0.90 @ 1.20 gives f* = 0.40 → quarter-Kelly = 10% of bankroll on one pick. The 2% cap would always bind, so the cap IS the policy. Simplify: FLAT 1.0–1.5% per pick (default 1.5%).
  • Caps: max 5 open picks; 4% per match; 15% of daily volume per league; same-match picks = one exposure.
  • Losing-streak math (plan for it): at p = 0.88, expected longest losing streak ≈ ln(N)/ln(1/0.12): ~3 over 300 picks, ~4 over 1500. At flat 1.5% that is a 4.5–6% drawdown from streaks alone — normal operation, printed on the dashboard so nobody panics into changing the system.
  • Circuit breakers: pause when rolling-50-pick hit rate < weighted break-even +1%, or rolling-30-pick CLV < 0, or drawdown > 10%, or live band-ECE > 2× backtest.

🚫8.3 · Accumulator policy — SINGLES ONLY v3.0

  • Safe picks invite acca temptation ("three 90% picks = easy 1.7×!"). Reality: hit rate compounds down (0.9³ = 73%) and any leg below its price floor drags the whole ticket −EV.
  • The core product publishes singles only.
  • A "double builder" MAY ship in the secondary module later — only combining picks that INDIVIDUALLY passed every gate, variance-labeled, never in the core list.

🔁8.4 · Pick lifecycle

generated→ gates (8.1)→ published with price floor→ re-checked each poll + lineup re-check→ taken / EXPIRED→ settled→ P&L · CLV · hit rate → reports
8.5 · Thin-week rule: the default answer in a thin week is "fewer picks", never "lower thresholds". ZERO PICKS IS A CORRECT OUTPUT and the UI treats it as a first-class state.  ·  8.6 · Transparency: every pick links to its full audit trail — probability, floor, edge, completeness, cell history, and its contribution to the rolling hit rate. Trust in a safe-picks product IS the hit-rate ledger.

📦8.8 · Secondary modules — everything that used to be co-primary

  • VALUE tier (+EV mid-odds hunting, 1.60–2.60): the complete v2.0 value engine remains implemented in valuebets/ but ships DISABLED. Enable in Phase 6 for users who opt in; its picks live on a separate page, never mixed into the safe list.
  • Exotic markets (BTTS, O2.5, corners/cards), draw specialist, deep learning, in-play: Phase 6+ experiments behind flags.
  • Secondary modules may not consume the core's API budget or delay its jobs (Celery queues separated: core vs secondary).
09

Backtesting Framework — Build Before Believing Anything

🧮Engine, protocol & honesty

  • Vectorized pandas engine over historical matches + odds (football-data.co.uk closing odds sufficient for MVP).
  • Walk-forward: train < S, predict season S, run the FULL gate pipeline (8.1) + flat staking, settle, compound. Never random folds.
  • Odds at prediction time (or closing as conservative proxy) minus 1–2% slippage; void/postponed handling; as-of assertions on every feature.
  • Outputs: equity curve, ROI/pick, hit rate per cell, EXPIRED rate, drawdown, CLV distribution, band-ECE reliability plots, bootstrap p-value of profit vs luck.

🩸Slow-bleed detector v3.0 — the safe product's own alarm

  • For every rolling 200-pick window compute ROI and its bootstrap 95% CI. Alarm when the CI upper bound < +0.5%: the feed may be hitting 88% and still bleeding — this catches it in ~2 months instead of a year. Runs in backtest AND live (§10.2 daily job).
  • When alarmed, decompose the bleed source: hit-rate shortfall vs price shortfall (avg odds taken below floor+margin) vs cell mix.

🗺️Per-league/cell decomposition report — writes league_metric

(a) model log-loss, (b) closing-line log-loss, (c) MBI = (b) − (a), plus hit rate, avg odds, yield per cell. Ranked by MBI this table IS the strategy; it flips market_rule cells on/off quarterly.

Promotion rule: a model/config ships ONLY if it beats the champion on band-ECE AND cell MBI AND flat-stake yield over ≥ 2 full seasons across ≥ 3 leagues.

Honest Success Criteria — Safe-First

🛡️Product

  • ≥ 88% hit rate on the published safe list over 300+ settled picks
  • EXPIRED rate 10–30% — proves the floor is doing its job
  • Zero picks published that failed any gate (audited)

🎯Calibration

  • Live band-ECE < 0.02 on [0.80, 0.97]
  • Live-vs-backtest ratio < 2× at all times

💵Economics

  • +2–5% ROI per settled pick at flat stakes
  • Slow-bleed detector green (rolling-200 ROI CI excludes ≤ +0.5%)
  • Max drawdown < 10% · positive CLV on ≥ 55% of taken picks

🗺️League strategy & engineering

  • Every enabled cell justified by positive MBI + yield + band-ECE < 0.02 over ≥ 2 seasons
  • The /leagues page can explain WHY any cell is on or off
  • Full audit trail; zero-leakage verified; one-click reviewed promotion
Discipline: the system publishes NOTHING on most thin days, retracts picks on bad lineups, and lets floors expire unmet. That restraint is the product.

Failure Modes & Anti-Patterns

How these systems actually die — each with its built-in defense. #11 and #12 are the v3.0 safe-product additions.

01Data leakage

Post-kickoff data in features: spectacular backtests, ruinous live. Defense: frozen snapshots, as-of test assertions.

02Backtest overfitting

Thresholds tuned till history looks great. Defense: walk-forward only, ≥2 seasons × ≥3 leagues, bootstrap p-values, holdout seasons.

03Odds availability bias

Backtesting prices you could not have taken. Defense: slippage haircut, closing-as-proxy, paper-trade comparison.

04Ignoring negative CLV

Bankroll up on luck while CLV is negative. Defense: CLV circuit breaker trumps the equity curve.

05Chasing the tail

"8% edge" on a 9.0 longshot = tail miscalibration. Defense: 7.0 ceiling + calibration-range gate.

06Quota pressure

Publishing because users expect picks today. Defense: thin-week rule; zero picks is a designed state.

07Soft-data mirage

"Predictable" low league that is thin data or a fixed match. Defense: completeness gate, integrity blacklist, MBI on adequate samples.

08Staking drift

Overriding stakes after wins/losses. Defense: computed flat stakes, logged; manual bets flagged separately.

09Silent model rot

League regime changes outdate the model. Defense: quarterly LPI/MBI review, live band-ECE monitor, champion/challenger.

10Infra debt at the worst time

Odds polling dies on matchday. Defense: job_run audits, Sentry alerts on missed beats, publish-time re-checks.

11SLOW BLEED v3.0 — THE safe-product killer

88% hit rate, money quietly gone: average odds crept below the floor+margin, or calibration slipped 2 points at 0.90. Defense: price floors on every pick, band-ECE gate, slow-bleed detector with bootstrap CI, bleed-source decomposition.

12Accumulator temptation v3.0

Combining "sure things" until the ticket is −EV and high-variance. Defense: singles-only core (§8.3); the UI never offers combination in the core list.

Glossary

1X2
Match result market: Home (1), Draw (X), Away (2).
Acca
Accumulator/parlay — multiplied-odds multi-leg bet. Core product: singles only (§8.3).
AH
Asian Handicap; AH −1.0 on heavy favorites = safe cell S4 (push insurance at −1.0 exactly).
Band-ECE
Expected Calibration Error measured ONLY inside the operating band [0.80, 0.97] — the safe product's core model metric (§6.3).
Break-even
1/odds — the hit rate below which a price loses money.
CLV
Closing Line Value — % by which taken odds beat the close. Best long-run profit predictor.
DC
Double Chance (1X / X2) — safe cell S1.
De-vig
Removing bookmaker margin to recover true implied probabilities (Shin's method).
DNB
Draw No Bet — stake refunded on draw; safe cell S2.
Edge
p_model_calibrated − p_market_devig.
EV
p × (odds−1) − (1−p) per unit staked.
EXPIRED
Pick whose price floor was never met before kickoff — a healthy outcome, tracked as a product metric.
Flat staking
Fixed % of bankroll per pick (core default 1.5%) — replaces Kelly at odds-on prices where caps always bind.
LPI
League Predictability Index (0–100, [ODDS §5]); ≥ 60 = whitelisted.
MBI
Market Beatability Index = closing-line log-loss minus model log-loss per (league, market) cell; positive = harvestable edge (§0.5).
O/U
Over/Under total goals; O1.5 = safe cell S3.
Price floor
min_acceptable_odds = (1+m)/p_cal — published with every pick; below it, no bet (§0.2, §7).
Slow bleed
Losing money at a high hit rate via bad prices or small calibration drift — failure mode #11.
Steam move
Sharp-book line move from informed money.
Vig / Margin
Bookmaker profit built into prices.
xG
Expected Goals — shot-quality scoring model.
