0. HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This file is DB seed data with commentary. Pipeline:
   1. Seed §3 into market_rule (odds windows, min_prob, min_edge, floor
      margin, league whitelist) and §8 into risk_profile.
   2. Backtest every (league, market) SAFE CELL through the full gate
      pipeline. Keep cells with positive MBI AND positive flat-stake
      yield AND band-ECE < 0.02 over >= 2 seasons. Expect a SPARSE grid
      (10-25 live cells worldwide) — that is the product working.
   3. Re-run the LPI + MBI cell review quarterly. Rules live in the DB,
      edited in Django admin — never hard-coded.

1. THE SAFETY DOCTRINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1.1 The break-even wall (BE = 1/odds)
        odds    BE hit rate      odds    BE hit rate
        1.05      95.2%          1.25      80.0%
        1.10      90.9%          1.30      76.9%
        1.15      87.0%          1.35      74.1%
        1.20      83.3%          1.40      71.4%
      A 90%-accurate feed is +6.2%/bet at avg odds 1.18 and -1.0%/bet
      at 1.10. Safety without price discipline = slow bleed.

  1.2 The slow-bleed trap
      "Safe tips" products die by winning 88% of bets while losing money
      invisibly: each win returns pennies, each loss eats many wins, and
      nobody notices for a year. This app's triple defense:
        * PRICE FLOOR on every pick (1.3)
        * DE-VIG EDGE GATE even on safe picks (master §8.1)
        * SLOW-BLEED DETECTOR — rolling 200-pick ROI bootstrap CI
          (master §9) in backtest and live.

  1.3 The price floor — published with every pick
        min_odds = (1 + m) / p_cal
      p_cal = calibrated probability; m = required margin per market
      (§3). Examples at m = 3%:
        p_cal 0.94 -> floor 1.096     p_cal 0.86 -> floor 1.198
        p_cal 0.90 -> floor 1.144     p_cal 0.82 -> floor 1.256
      If the market never offers the floor before kickoff, the pick
      EXPIRES unbet. EXPIRED is a healthy outcome (target 10-30% of
      picks) — it proves the floor is doing its job.

  1.4 Favorite-longshot bias — now works FOR us
      Bookmakers under-shade favorites and over-shade longshots. The
      safe product lives entirely on the favorite side of that bias:
      the least-taxed prices in the book. Margin also concentrates on
      draws/outsiders, so de-vigging (Shin) flatters exactly our side.
      This is the structural reason a safe-first product is viable
      at all.

2. THE SAFE MARKET RANKING (THE PRODUCT'S MARKETS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  S1 — DOUBLE CHANCE 1X (dominant home favorite)      THE FLAGSHIP
     What: home win OR draw, on a top club at home vs a bottom-half
           side in a dominant-team league (§5).
     Why safe: bundles 2 of 3 outcomes behind the strongest single
           force in soccer (elite home favorite). p_cal 0.88-0.95.
     Odds window: 1.10 - 1.28   | min_prob 0.88 | floor margin 1.5-3%
     What breaks it: rotation (cup/Europe next midweek), dead rubbers,
           early red cards (irreducible), freak keeper errors.
           => rotation-risk flag + lineup-confirmed re-check are gates.
     Expected hit rate: 89-94% on enabled cells.

  S2 — DRAW NO BET (same fixture profile)             THE CLEANEST
     What: favorite to win; stake refunded on draw.
     Why safe: the draw — the modeler's nightmare — is neutralized to a
           push instead of a loss. Effective p (win|not void) 0.84-0.92.
     Odds window: 1.18 - 1.45   | min_prob 0.85 | floor margin 2-3%
     What breaks it: same as S1 minus draw risk; upsets only.
     Note: cleaner than S1 for CLV and MBI measurement (2 outcomes).
     Expected hit rate: 85-91% (voids excluded).

  S3 — OVER 1.5 GOALS (scoring leagues only)          THE VOLUME CELL
     What: 2+ total goals in leagues with structurally high scoring
           (Eredivisie, Bundesliga, Austria, Switzerland, Norway...).
     Why safe: independent of who wins — immune to upsets, rotation
           hurts it less; league scoring rates are highly stable
           season-over-season. p_cal 0.78-0.86 in the right leagues.
     Odds window: 1.18 - 1.38   | min_prob 0.80 | floor margin 3%
     What breaks it: derby/relegation cagey matches, extreme weather,
           park-the-bus mismatches (big favorite + massed defense can
           yield 1-0). => style-matchup features gate it.
     Expected hit rate: 80-86% on enabled cells.

  S4 — ASIAN HANDICAP -1.0 (heavy favorites)          THE INSURED WIN
     What: favorite -1.0; win by 2+ = win, win by exactly 1 = PUSH
           (stake back), draw/loss = loss.
     Why safe: the push at -1.0 exactly insures the most common
           "favorite underperforms" outcome (1-0). Effective
           win ~0.55-0.65 + push ~0.15-0.20 => loss rate only ~0.20-0.28.
     Odds window: 1.35 - 1.55   | min_prob 0.72 eff. | floor margin 3%
     What breaks it: favorite coasting at 1-0 (game-state management).
     Expected: ~60% win / ~18% push / ~22% loss on enabled cells.
     Role: the highest-ROI member of the basket; smallest allocation.

  S5 — ASIAN HANDICAP +1.5 (underdog side)            THE MIRROR (v3.1)
     What: back the underdog +1.5 in BALANCED-to-moderate fixtures;
           wins on a dog win, a draw, or a 1-goal loss — the favorite
           must win by 2+ to beat it.
     Why safe: in mid-table fixtures favorites win by 2+ only ~20-28%
           of the time => cover ~72-80%. Structural bonus: AH is the
           LOWEST-margin market in soccer (~2-2.5% at sharps) with the
           sharpest closing lines of any market — CLV/MBI measurement
           runs at full power here. Fully backtestable from scores.
     Odds window: 1.25 - 1.45   | min_prob 0.75 | floor margin 3%
     What breaks it: the dead-favorite romp (early goal -> collapse);
           a dog with nothing to play for. NOTE: the fixture-profile
           gate is the OPPOSITE of S1 — this cell wants balanced games.
     Expected hit rate: 72-80% on enabled cells.

  S6 — UNDER 3.5 GOALS (chronic unders leagues)       THE UNDERS TWIN (v3.1)
     What: 3 or fewer total goals, ONLY in structurally low-scoring
           leagues (FRA Ligue 2, ITA Serie B, ESP LaLiga 2, Greece).
     Why safe: base rate ~75-82% there (vs only 62-72% league-wide in
           the Big-5); result-independent like S3 — immune to upsets;
           the DC goal matrix already prices it for free.
     Odds window: 1.20 - 1.32   | min_prob 0.78 | floor margin 3%
     What breaks it: rare 4+ goal blowouts; late-season dead rubbers
           going over (the dead-rubber flag already gates the core).
     Expected hit rate: 78-84% on enabled cells.

  S1 VARIANT (v3.1): DC X2 on AWAY favorites — the away sibling of S1,
     hits ~85-90% @ 1.15-1.35 in dominant-team leagues. Seeded as S1c.

  EXCLUDED FROM THE CORE (despite high hit rates):
   * Over 0.5 goals — hits ~92-93% BUT prices 1.02-1.07 sit behind the
     break-even wall (needs >93-98%); retail prices never clear a floor.
   * Anything priced 1.01-1.09 — same wall; also stake-limited.
   * "Favorite to advance" (cup ties) — rotation chaos; secondary only.
   * Raw draws, correct score, HT-FT — not safe, not secondary-safe;
     see §9/§10.

  2.5 THE FIVE TESTS (v3.1) — every candidate market must pass ALL:
     1. BREAK-EVEN CLEARANCE — a calibrated probability achievable at
        odds that clear the price floor (kills O0.5, U4.5, 1.01-1.09).
     2. MARGIN — market overround <= ~5%. Main markets run ~3-7% (2.5%
        at sharps); niche/prop books run 15-30%+ — the vig wall no
        model can climb (kills corners, props).
     3. DATA & SETTLEMENT — backtestable history + consistent official
        counts (kills throw-ins: no standard data feeds exist at all).
     4. LIQUIDITY & LIMITS — real stake sizes, survivable account
        longevity, a sharp closing line to measure CLV/MBI against.
     5. INTEGRITY — no single-actor-controllable micro-events (kills
        throw-in / first-X props — the classic spot-fixing surface;
        cf. the Le Tissier 1995 first-throw-in case; integrity bodies
        flag exactly these low-visibility micro-markets).
     S1-S6 pass all five. Most niche markets fail two or more — and
     usually on dimensions no modeling skill can fix.

  2.6 WATCHLIST (candidate cells for the M4/G1 backtest to judge —
      NOT seeded live):
     * FIRST-HALF OVER 0.5 in scoring leagues — ~65-70% of matches
       have a first-half goal league-wide (~72-78% in the GER/NED
       tier) @ ~1.22-1.35: borderline vs the wall, and half markets
       carry worse margins than full-match totals. Model must select
       well above base rate to clear a floor.
     * HOME-FAVORITE TEAM TOTAL O0.5 ("favorite to score") — top clubs
       at home score in ~88-93% of matches, but priced 1.06-1.15:
       mostly wall-blocked; occasionally clears for away favorites.
     * UCL/UEL GROUP-STAGE MISMATCH (v3.2) — S1-profile fixtures
       (pot-1 club at home vs pot-4 minnow) on TUE-THU: extends the
       weekly coverage map [§7.1] into midweek. Gates as S1a PLUS
       strict dead-rubber (late group stage, qualification sealed) and
       rotation checks. Data caveat: not on football-data.co.uk —
       needs API-Football history + an odds archive before it can even
       be backtested => watchlist, not seed.

  2.7 RESEARCHED & REJECTED (2026-07 research round):
     * UNDER 4.5 GOALS — ~90%+ hit rate but priced 1.04-1.12: behind
       the wall, same story as Over 0.5.
     * BTTS NO — ~40-55% base rates even in friendly leagues: a value
       market, not a safe one.
     * WIN EITHER HALF / TO WIN A HALF — hit rates in the 70s but
       prop-book margins and no sharp benchmark.
     * CORNERS (O/U, handicaps) — genuinely modelable (negative
       binomial over counts; corners ARE in football-data.co.uk) and
       academically shown mildly beatable in-league, BUT: niche-market
       overrounds run 15-30%+, limits are tiny, settlement counts vary
       by provider, and closing lines are not sharp => fails tests 2 &
       4. Phase 6 in-play research topic at most [§9.4].
     * CARDS / BOOKINGS — referee assignment is a real modelable
       signal, but heavy margins + officiating volatility +
       manipulation exposure (deliberate-booking scandals). Phase 6
       research at most.
     * THROW-INS & first-X micro props (first throw-in / first corner /
       first booking) — HARD EXCLUSION, all tiers, forever: no data
       feeds, no liquidity, and the canonical spot-fixing surface — a
       single player controls the event without affecting the match.
       Fails tests 2, 3, 4 AND 5.

4. PRICE DISCIPLINE: TIMING, LINE SHOPPING, BOOKMAKERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4.1 Odds-on price behavior
     * Prices on popular favorites usually SHORTEN toward kickoff as
       public money piles on. => default capture window for S1/S2/S4 is
       EARLY: T-3d to T-24h. O1.5 (S3) prices are stabler; capture
       window per cell from backtest ("price at T-24h vs closing").
     * Line shopping matters MORE at odds-on: the gap between 1.14 and
       1.18 on the same selection is the entire profit margin. Best
       price across books per selection, always; availability haircut
       1-2% in backtests.

  4.2 Bookmaker landscape
     SHARP (truth signal + volume): Pinnacle (lowest margins, welcomes
       winners; closing line = calibration benchmark), Betfair Exchange
       (no margin, commission; thin early liquidity in small leagues —
       relevant since our leagues ARE small: check liquidity per cell).
     SOFT (price capture until limited): bet365, William Hill, Betway,
       Unibet, 888, locals. They lag sharp moves and shade favorites
       less aggressively during promos => floors met more often. They
       limit winners; treat account longevity as a managed resource.
     Classify every book: is_sharp + limits_winners flags.

  4.3 Consensus & steam
     De-vig every book (Shin) -> consensus prob weighted toward sharps.
     Sharp-vs-soft divergence > 4-5% = alert + feature. For the safe
     product a sharp move AGAINST our pick (favorite drifting) is a
     warning signal that feeds the re-check, not just an opportunity.

5. LEAGUE WHITELIST FOR THE SAFE PRODUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The safe product needs leagues where (a) top-vs-bottom mismatches are
  extreme and frequent (S1/S2/S4) or (b) scoring is structurally high
  and stable (S3) — with adequate data and clean integrity. Base rates
  = approximate multi-season ranges (Home/Draw/Away | Over 2.5).

  5.1 DOMINANT-TEAM LEAGUES — feed S1/S2/S4 (the core asset)
   * SCOTLAND Premiership [SC0]       (~40/24/36 | ~50-53%)
       Celtic/Rangers vs bottom half = Europe's most certain fixtures.
       Away% inflated BY the Old Firm winning away — model the split.
   * PORTUGAL Primeira Liga [P1]      (~43/25/32 | ~47-51%)
       Big-3 dominance; softer market than Big-5.
   * NETHERLANDS Eredivisie [N1]      (~46/23/31 | ~56-62%)
       DOUBLE ASSET: top-3 dominance (S1/S2) AND Europe's most reliable
       scoring league (S3a). The single most valuable league for this
       product.
   * AUSTRIA Bundesliga [extra]       (~44/24/32 | ~55-58%)
       Salzburg dominance + high scoring (S1+S3a). Playoff-split format
       flag required.
   * SWITZERLAND Super League [extra] (~43/25/32 | ~55-58%)
       Similar double profile; YB/Basel cycles.
   * GREECE Super League [G1]         (~43/26/31 | ~42-46%)
       Big-4 dominance + strong home edge => S1/S2 only (LOW scoring —
       never S3). Integrity: monitor closely, stricter gates.
   * TURKEY Super Lig [T1]            (~45/24/31 | ~51-55%)
       Istanbul dominance + strongest home edges. S1/S2; volatile
       officiating — completeness and integrity gates strict.
   (CROATIA HNL / CZECH Fortuna — Dinamo/Slavia-Sparta dominance fits
    S1 but data is thin: watchlist until completeness proves out.)

  5.2 SCORING LEAGUES — feed S3
   * GERMANY Bundesliga [D1]          (O2.5 ~53-58%) — elite data; also
       the Big-5 exception worth safe cells (Bayern-era S1a fixtures).
   * NETHERLANDS Eredivisie [N1]      (see 5.1 — S3a anchor)
   * AUSTRIA [extra] / SWITZERLAND [extra] (S3a)
   * NORWAY Eliteserien [extra]       (O2.5 ~55-60%) — SUMMER: keeps the
       product alive June-August.
   * SWEDEN Allsvenskan [extra]       (O2.5 ~53-57%) — summer partner.
   * GERMANY 2. Bundesliga [D2]       (O2.5 ~53-58%) — softer market.

  5.3 BIG-5 STATUS (demoted for this product)
   * ENGLAND [E0], SPAIN [SP1], ITALY [I1], FRANCE [F1]: sharpest
     markets — safe-cell floors rarely met at +EV. Role: DATA MASS for
     training, CALIBRATION BENCHMARK (if the model shows big safe edges
     in the EPL, suspect a bug), and occasional S1a fixtures for the
     truly dominant sides. GERMANY [D1] is the exception (5.2).

  5.4 LPI — League Predictability Index (unchanged formula)
     LPI = 25*imbalance + 15*(1-draw_rate_norm) + 15*style_stability
         + 10*(1-churn) + 15*data_depth + 10*market_softness
         + 10*integrity
     Cutoffs: >= 60 whitelisted | 45-59 training-data only | < 45 watch.
     SAFE-FIRST WEIGHTING NOTE: for S1/S2/S4 cells, imbalance and
     integrity dominate in practice; for S3 cells, style_stability
     does. The cell-level MBI (master §0.5/§9) makes the final call —
     LPI qualifies a league, MBI enables a cell.

6. STAKING & DRAWDOWN FOR ODDS-ON BETTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6.1 FLAT staking (the v3.0 default)
     Kelly at odds-on prices produces absurd fractions (p=0.90 @ 1.20
     -> f* = 0.40; quarter-Kelly = 10% of bankroll on one pick). Any
     sane cap binds on every pick, so the cap IS the policy:
       FLAT 1.0-1.5% of bankroll per pick. Default 1.5%.
     Caps: max 5 open picks; 4% per match; 15% of daily volume per
     league; same-match picks = one exposure.

  6.2 Losing-streak math (print it on the dashboard)
     At p = 0.88 (q = 0.12), expected LONGEST losing streak over N
     picks ~ ln(N)/ln(1/q):
       N = 300  -> ~3 consecutive losses
       N = 1500 -> ~4 consecutive losses
     At flat 1.5% that is a 4.5-6% drawdown from streaks alone —
     NORMAL OPERATION. Panicking into threshold changes after a normal
     streak is failure mode #8 (staking drift).

  6.3 Circuit breakers (risk_profile)
     Pause publishing when ANY of:
       * rolling-50-pick hit rate < weighted break-even + 1%
       * rolling-30-pick CLV < 0
       * drawdown > 10%
       * live band-ECE > 2x backtest
     Resume requires human review of the bleed-source decomposition.

  6.4 Accumulators — SINGLES ONLY in the core
     0.90^3 = 0.73: three "sure things" make one coin-flip-plus ticket,
     and one leg below its floor drags the whole ticket -EV while the
     margin compounds. The core list never combines. (A strict double
     builder MAY appear in the secondary module, Phase 6, variance-
     labeled, only from picks that individually passed every gate.)

      * Publish at most 1-2 picks per day: the HIGHEST-p_cal
        qualifying picks across all enabled cells that day.
      * Rationale: at odds-on prices a single loss wipes ~4 wins, so
        a day is green only if EVERY pick wins (for < 5 picks/day —
        see 7.3). Fewer, stronger picks per day maximizes green days:
        the exact opposite of the "more picks = more winning" instinct.
      * Zero-pick days still exist and remain correct output; they
        are excluded from the cadence KPI's denominator (they are
        no-bet days, not red days).

  7.3 GREEN-DAY MATH (why picks-per-day is the lever)
      Green day = positive P&L day. At avg odds ~1.25 a day needs
      > 80% of its picks to win => for 1-4 picks/day ALL must win.
      Green-day probability g = p^n (p = per-pick hit rate, n =
      picks that day):
                     n=1      n=2      n=3
        p=0.92       0.92     0.85     0.78
        p=0.88       0.88     0.77     0.68
      Weekly (7 betting days, binomial), P(>= 5 green days):
        g=0.92 -> ~99%    g=0.88 -> ~96%    g=0.85 -> ~93%
        g=0.80 -> ~85%    g=0.75 -> ~76%    g=0.70 -> ~65%
      => The cadence goal is reachable with 1(-2) picks/day drawn
         from the S1/S2 end of the basket (p_cal >= 0.88-0.92).
         It is NOT reachable by adding volume: 3 picks/day at p=0.88
         gives g=0.68 => expected ~4.8 green days and a sub-5 week
         roughly 1 time in 3.

  7.4 THE CADENCE KPI (the honest form of "win 5 days a week")
      * TARGET: >= 5 green days per week in >= 85-90% of full-coverage
        weeks (7 betting days), tracked on /ledger as a rolling
        12-week green-day rate.
      * NOT A GUARANTEE: even at g = 0.88, roughly 1 week in 25 has
        <= 4 green days, and losses cluster (§6.2 streak math). A 3-4
        green-day week with intact gates is VARIANCE, not failure —
        the correct response is nothing. The circuit breakers (§6.3)
        exist for real drift; the calendar does not.
      * The KPI may NEVER be met by: lowering min_prob, shaving floor
        margins, forcing picks on thin days, or double-dipping a
        match. It is met by widening COVERAGE (7.1) and sharpening
        CALIBRATION — both evidence-gated.
      * Weekly P&L, not daily, remains the economic truth: a 6-green-
        day week can still be net-negative if the one red day held the
        week's largest-stake or worst-priced pick. The daily ledger
        exists for trust; the weekly ledger for money.
