The Safety Doctrine NEW v3.0

🧱1.1 · The break-even wall (BE = 1/odds)

OddsBE hit rateOddsBE hit rate
1.0595.2%1.2580.0%
1.1090.9%1.3076.9%
1.1587.0%1.3574.1%
1.2083.3%1.4071.4%

A 90%-accurate feed is +6.2%/bet at avg odds 1.18 and −1.0%/bet at 1.10. Safety without price discipline = slow bleed.

🩸1.2 · The slow-bleed trap

"Safe tips" products die by winning 88% of bets while losing money invisibly: each win returns pennies, each loss eats many wins, nobody notices for a year. Triple defense:

  • Price floor on every pick (1.3)
  • De-vig edge gate even on safe picks (master §8.1)
  • Slow-bleed detector — rolling 200-pick ROI bootstrap CI (master §9), backtest AND live

📏1.3 · The price floor — published with every pick

min_odds = (1 + m) / p_cal Examples at m = 3%: p_cal 0.94 → floor 1.096 · p_cal 0.90 → floor 1.144 · p_cal 0.86 → floor 1.198 · p_cal 0.82 → floor 1.256

If the market never offers the floor before kickoff, the pick EXPIRES unbet. EXPIRED is a healthy outcome (target 10–30% of picks) — it proves the floor is doing its job.

🎯1.4 · Favorite-longshot bias — now works FOR us

Bookmakers under-shade favorites and over-shade longshots. The safe product lives entirely on the favorite side of that bias: the least-taxed prices in the book. Margin also concentrates on draws/outsiders, so de-vigging (Shin) flatters exactly our side. This is the structural reason a safe-first product is viable at all.

02

The Safe Market Ranking THE PRODUCT'S MARKETS

S1

👑Double Chance 1X THE FLAGSHIP

What: home win OR draw, on a top club at home vs a bottom-half side in a dominant-team league (§5).

Why safe: bundles 2 of 3 outcomes behind the strongest single force in soccer — the elite home favorite.

odds 1.10–1.28 min_prob 0.88 floor m 1.5–3% hit target 89–94%

What breaks it: rotation (cup/Europe next midweek), dead rubbers, early red cards (irreducible), freak keeper errors ⇒ rotation-risk flag + lineup-confirmed re-check are gates.

S2

🧿Draw No Bet THE CLEANEST

What: favorite to win; stake refunded on a draw.

Why safe: the draw — the modeler's nightmare — is neutralized to a push instead of a loss.

odds 1.18–1.45 min_prob 0.85 floor m 2–3% hit target 85–91% (voids excl.)

Note: cleaner than S1 for CLV and MBI measurement (two outcomes). Breaks like S1, minus draw risk — upsets only.

S3

⚽Over 1.5 Goals THE VOLUME CELL

What: 2+ total goals, in leagues with structurally high scoring (Eredivisie, Bundesliga, Austria, Switzerland, Norway…).

Why safe: independent of who wins — immune to upsets; rotation hurts it less; league scoring rates are highly stable season-over-season.

odds 1.18–1.38 min_prob 0.80 floor m 3% hit target 80–86%

What breaks it: cagey derbies/relegation matches, extreme weather, park-the-bus mismatches (1-0 grinds) ⇒ style-matchup features gate it.

S4

🛡️Asian Handicap −1.0 THE INSURED WIN

What: favorite −1.0; win by 2+ = win, win by exactly 1 = PUSH (stake back), draw/loss = loss.

Why safe: the push at −1.0 exactly insures the most common "favorite underperforms" outcome (1-0).

odds 1.35–1.55 min_prob 0.72 eff. floor m 3% ~60%W / 18%P / 22%L

Role: the highest-ROI member of the basket; smallest allocation. Breaks: favorite coasting at 1-0 (game-state management).

S5

🪞Asian Handicap +1.5 (underdog) THE MIRROR · v3.1

What: back the underdog +1.5 in balanced-to-moderate fixtures; wins on a dog win, a draw, or a 1-goal loss — the favorite must win by 2+ to beat it.

Why safe: in mid-table fixtures favorites win by 2+ only ~20–28% of the time ⇒ cover ~72–80%. Structural bonus: AH is the lowest-margin market in soccer (~2–2.5% at sharps) with the sharpest closing lines — CLV/MBI measurement runs at full power. Fully backtestable from scores.

odds 1.25–1.45 min_prob 0.75 floor m 3% hit target 72–80%

What breaks it: the dead-favorite romp (early goal → collapse); a dog with nothing to play for. The fixture-profile gate is the opposite of S1 — this cell wants balanced games.

S6

🧊Under 3.5 Goals THE UNDERS TWIN · v3.1

What: 3 or fewer total goals, ONLY in structurally low-scoring leagues (Ligue 2, Serie B, LaLiga 2, Greece).

Why safe: base rate ~75–82% there (vs only 62–72% league-wide in the Big-5); result-independent like S3 — immune to upsets; the DC goal matrix already prices it for free.

odds 1.20–1.32 min_prob 0.78 floor m 3% hit target 78–84%

What breaks it: rare 4+ goal blowouts; late-season dead rubbers going over (the dead-rubber flag already gates the core).

S1 variant (v3.1): DC X2 on AWAY favorites — the away sibling of S1, hits ~85–90% @ 1.15–1.35 in dominant-team leagues. Seeded as S1c. S5/S6 are candidates: they enter the live product only via G1 cell evidence, like every other cell.
Excluded from the core despite high hit rates: Over 0.5 goals (hits ~92–93% BUT prices 1.02–1.07 sit behind the break-even wall — retail prices never clear a floor) · anything priced 1.01–1.09 (same wall + stake limits) · "favorite to advance" cup ties (rotation chaos — secondary only) · raw draws, correct score, HT-FT (not safe, not secondary-safe; §9/§10).

2.5 · The Five Tests v3.1 — every candidate market must pass ALL five

  • BREAK-EVEN CLEARANCE — a calibrated probability achievable at odds that clear the price floor (kills O0.5, U4.5, anything 1.01–1.09).
  • MARGIN — market overround ≤ ~5%. Main markets run ~3–7% (2.5% at sharps); niche/prop books run 15–30%+ — a vig wall no model can climb (kills corners, props).
  • DATA & SETTLEMENT — backtestable history + consistent official counts (kills throw-ins: no standard data feeds exist at all).
  • LIQUIDITY & LIMITS — real stake sizes, survivable account longevity, and a sharp closing line to measure CLV/MBI against.
  • INTEGRITY — no single-actor-controllable micro-events (kills throw-in / first-X props — the classic spot-fixing surface; cf. the Le Tissier 1995 first-throw-in case).

S1–S6 pass all five. Most niche markets fail two or more — usually on dimensions no modeling skill can fix.

👀2.6 · Watchlist for M4/G1 to judge — NOT seeded live

  • First-half Over 0.5 in scoring leagues — ~65–70% base league-wide (~72–78% in the GER/NED tier) @ ~1.22–1.35: borderline vs the wall, and half markets carry worse margins than full-match totals. The model must select well above base rate to clear a floor.
  • Home-favorite team total O0.5 ("favorite to score") — top clubs at home score in ~88–93% of matches, but priced 1.06–1.15: mostly wall-blocked; occasionally clears for away favorites.
  • UCL/UEL group-stage mismatch v3.2 — S1-profile fixtures (pot-1 club at home vs pot-4 minnow) on TUE–THU: extends the weekly coverage map (§7.1) into midweek. Gates as S1a PLUS strict dead-rubber (qualification sealed) and rotation checks. Data caveat: not on football-data.co.uk — needs API-Football history + an odds archive before it can even be backtested ⇒ watchlist, not seed.

🗑️2.7 · Researched & rejected 2026-07 research round

  • Under 4.5 goals — ~90%+ hit rate but priced 1.04–1.12: behind the wall.
  • BTTS No — ~40–55% base rates: a value market, not a safe one.
  • Win either half — 70s hit rates but prop-book margins, no sharp benchmark.
  • Corners — modelable (negative binomial; counts ARE in football-data.co.uk) and academically shown mildly beatable in-league, BUT niche overrounds run 15–30%+, limits tiny, settlement counts vary by provider, closing lines not sharp ⇒ fails tests 2 & 4. Phase 6 in-play research at most [§9.4].
  • Cards/bookings — referee assignment is a real signal, but heavy margins + officiating volatility + manipulation exposure (deliberate-booking scandals). Phase 6 at most.
  • Throw-ins & first-X micro props — HARD EXCLUSION, all tiers, forever: no data feeds, no liquidity, and the canonical spot-fixing surface — a single player controls the event without affecting the match. Fails tests 2, 3, 4 AND 5.

04

Price Discipline: Timing, Line Shopping, Bookmakers

⏱️4.1 · Odds-on price behavior

  • Prices on popular favorites usually shorten toward kickoff (public money) ⇒ default capture window for S1/S2/S4 is EARLY: T-3d to T-24h. O1.5 (S3) prices are stabler; window per cell from backtest.
  • Line shopping matters MORE at odds-on: the gap between 1.14 and 1.18 on the same selection is the entire profit margin. Best price across books, always; 1–2% availability haircut in backtests.

🏦4.2 · Bookmaker landscape

  • SHARP (truth signal + volume): Pinnacle (lowest margins, welcomes winners; closing line = calibration benchmark), Betfair Exchange (no margin, commission; check liquidity per cell — our leagues ARE small).
  • SOFT (price capture until limited): bet365, William Hill, Betway, Unibet, 888, locals — they lag sharps and shade favorites less during promos ⇒ floors met more often. They limit winners; manage account longevity.
  • Classify every book: is_sharp + limits_winners.

📡4.3 · Consensus & steam

  • De-vig every book (Shin) → consensus prob weighted toward sharps.
  • Sharp-vs-soft divergence > 4–5% = alert + feature.
  • For the safe product, a sharp move against our pick (favorite drifting) is a warning that feeds the re-check — not just an opportunity.

📐5.4 · LPI — League Predictability Index

LPI = 25·imbalance + 15·(1−draw_rate) + 15·style_stability + 10·(1−churn) + 15·data_depth + 10·softness + 10·integrity Cutoffs: ≥ 60 whitelisted · 45–59 training-data only · < 45 watch.

Safe-first weighting note: for S1/S2/S4 cells, imbalance and integrity dominate in practice; for S3 cells, style_stability does. The cell-level MBI makes the final call — LPI qualifies a league, MBI enables a cell.

Staking & Drawdown for Odds-On Betting REBUILT v3.0

⚖️6.1 · FLAT staking (the default)

Kelly at odds-on prices produces absurd fractions (p=0.90 @ 1.20 → f* = 0.40; quarter-Kelly = 10% of bankroll on one pick). Any sane cap binds on every pick, so the cap IS the policy:

FLAT 1.0–1.5% of bankroll per pick. Default 1.5%.

Caps: max 5 open picks · 4% per match · 15% of daily volume per league · same-match picks = one exposure.

📉6.2 · Losing-streak math (print it on the dashboard)

At p = 0.88 (q = 0.12), expected longest losing streak over N picks ≈ ln(N)/ln(1/q):

N picksExpected longest streak
300~3 consecutive losses
1500~4 consecutive losses

At flat 1.5% that is a 4.5–6% drawdown from streaks alone — normal operation. Panicking into threshold changes after a normal streak is failure mode #8 (staking drift).

🔌6.3 · Circuit breakers (risk_profile)

Pause publishing when ANY of:

  • rolling-50-pick hit rate < weighted break-even + 1%
  • rolling-30-pick CLV < 0
  • drawdown > 10%
  • live band-ECE > 2× backtest

Resume requires human review of the bleed-source decomposition.

🚫6.4 · Accumulators — SINGLES ONLY in the core

0.90³ = 0.73: three "sure things" make one coin-flip-plus ticket, and one leg below its floor drags the whole ticket −EV while the margin compounds. The core list never combines. (A strict double builder MAY appear in the secondary module, Phase 6, variance-labeled, only from picks that individually passed every gate.)

🃏7.2 · The Daily Card v3.2 operating mode

  • Publish at most 1–2 picks per day: the highest-p_cal qualifying picks across all enabled cells that day.
  • Rationale: at odds-on prices a single loss wipes ~4 wins, so a day is green only if EVERY pick wins (for <5 picks/day — see 7.3). Fewer, stronger picks per day maximizes green days — the exact opposite of the "more picks = more winning" instinct.
  • Zero-pick days still exist and remain correct output; they are excluded from the cadence KPI's denominator (no-bet days, not red days).

🧮7.3 · Green-Day Math v3.2

Green day = positive P&L day. At avg odds ~1.25 a day needs >80% of its picks to win ⇒ for 1–4 picks/day all must win. Green-day probability g = pⁿ:

p per pickn=1n=2n=3
0.920.920.850.78
0.880.880.770.68

Weekly (7 betting days, binomial), P(≥5 green days): g=0.92 → ~99% · g=0.88 → ~96% · g=0.85 → ~93% · g=0.80 → ~85% · g=0.75 → ~76% · g=0.70 → ~65%.

⇒ Reachable with 1(–2) picks/day from the S1/S2 end (p_cal ≥ 0.88–0.92). Not reachable by adding volume: 3 picks/day at p=0.88 gives g=0.68 ⇒ a sub-5 week roughly 1 time in 3.

📅7.4 · The Cadence KPI the honest form of "win 5 days a week"

  • TARGET: ≥ 5 green days per week in ≥ 85–90% of full-coverage weeks (7 betting days), tracked on /ledger as a rolling 12-week green-day rate.
  • NOT A GUARANTEE: even at g = 0.88, roughly 1 week in 25 has ≤ 4 green days, and losses cluster (§6.2 streak math). A 3–4 green-day week with intact gates is variance, not failure — the correct response is nothing. The circuit breakers (§6.3) exist for real drift; the calendar does not.
  • The KPI may never be met by: lowering min_prob, shaving floor margins, forcing picks on thin days, or double-dipping a match. It is met by widening coverage (7.1) and sharpening calibration — both evidence-gated.
  • Weekly P&L, not daily, remains the economic truth: a 6-green-day week can still be net-negative if the one red day held the week's worst-priced pick. The daily ledger exists for trust; the weekly ledger for money.

09

Secondary / Miscellaneous Phase 6+ · flag-gated · OFF by default

The former VALUE tier, compressed. Never mixed into the core list. Retained for when the safe product is stable and an opt-in VALUE module is wanted.

📊9.1 · Value odds bands (v2.0 summary)

  • 1.60–2.60 PRIME VALUE — thinnest margins, 3–6% edges realistic, hit rate 40–60%; most +EV profit lives here.
  • 2.60–4.00 SELECTIVE — edge ≥ 5–6%, half stakes.
  • 4.00–7.00 RARE — edge ≥ 8% + structural reason only.
  • > 7.00 NEVER (unchanged hard filter).
  • Draws: only via a dedicated specialist model — margin-heavy and model-hostile.

🎯9.2 · Value markets (quarter-Kelly, 2% cap)

  • 1X2 home fav 1.45–2.20 @3% · away fav 1.60–2.40 @3.5%
  • 1X2 away value 2.20–3.50 @5% (half stakes)
  • O/U 2.5 1.60–2.10 @3% · BTTS 1.55–2.10 @3%
  • AH quarter-lines 1.70–2.05 @3%
  • Correct score / HT-FT: never (margin monsters).

🌏9.3 · Expansion leagues (watchlist)

Enter only with 2+ profitable backtested seasons AND completeness proof: Belgium [B1] (playoff format), Denmark [extra], Croatia HNL, Czech Fortuna, Poland [extra], Japan J1 [extra] (good data, timezone spread), USA MLS [extra] (Overs only — parity kills 1X2), Brazil [extra] (churn/travel), Argentina [extra] (unders only, chaotic formats).

🔬9.4 · Research projects

Steam-lag capture, in-play, cards/corners, sequence models: behind flags; each needs its own backtest gate before touching real money. None may consume core API budget.

10

Hard Exclusions ALL TIERS

  • International friendlies — motivation unknowable. Never.
  • Club friendlies / pre-season — rotation lottery. Never.
