  Pipeline:
    1. Seed the thresholds in sections 2, 3, 12 into valuebets.RiskProfile
       and a new valuebets.MarketRule table (odds window, min edge, tier,

1. CORE PRINCIPLES — WHY ODDS BANDS ARE NOT EQUALLY BEATABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A) FAVORITE-LONGSHOT BIAS (the most documented inefficiency in sports
     betting): recreational money chases big payouts, so bookmakers
     shade longshot odds well below fair value and can price favorites
     close to fair. Consequences:
       * Short-priced favorites lose the LEAST to the margin.
       * Longshots (> ~7.0) are structurally the worst bets in soccer.
         Expected loss betting random longshots is several times worse
         than betting random favorites.
       * Model calibration is also weakest in the tails — so a "big edge"
         on a longshot is more likely a model error than a market error.

  B) MARGIN CONCENTRATION: the bookmaker's vig is not spread evenly
     across a market's selections — disproportionate margin sits on the
     draw and the outsider. De-vigging with Shin's method (master plan
     5.6) exposes this. Your value engine will naturally surface more
     edges on favorites and mid-range prices. Trust that pattern.

  C) TIMING — WHEN TO TAKE THE PRICE (CLV discipline):
     * Odds open ~5-7 days out at low limits, sharpen continuously, and
       are most accurate at kickoff (the closing line).
     * Soft bookmakers lag sharp moves by minutes-to-hours. The system's
       cheapest edge: detect a sharp-book move (Pinnacle/exchange) and
       take the stale price at a lagging soft book before it follows.
     * Favorites that the model likes are usually best taken EARLY
       (public money later shortens them); popular overs/big-club prices
       drift the other way. Backtest per market: "price taken at T-24h
       vs closing" tells you the optimal capture window empirically.
     * Track CLV on every bet (bet.clv in the schema). If picks
       consistently beat the close, profit follows; if CLV is negative,
       stop and fix the model — do not wait for the bankroll to prove it.

  D) LIQUIDITY & LIMITS REALITY: soft books limit or close winning
     accounts, often within weeks. Plan for it: spread volume across
     books, prefer exchanges (Betfair) and limit-friendly sharps
     (Pinnacle) as volume grows, and treat account longevity as a
     managed resource. This affects WHERE the best-odds engine should
     route (section 4).

2. ODDS BANDS — WHAT TO TARGET, WHAT TO AVOID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  BAND         VERDICT       EXPECTED    NOTES
                             HIT RATE
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1.01-1.09    AVOID            —        No room for error; one upset erases
                                         10-90 wins; margin eats everything;
                                         stake limits bite hardest here.
  1.10-1.35    SAFE TIER     78-92%      The "low risk" product: Double
                                         Chance, Draw No Bet, Over 0.5/1.5,
                                         heavy Asian Handicaps. Only with
                                         verified edge >= 1.5% — at these
                                         odds tiny mispricings decide
                                         profit vs slow bleed.
  1.35-1.60    SAFE/VALUE    62-75%      Strong favorites in stable leagues,
               OVERLAP                   DNB on away favorites, Over 1.5 in
                                         scoring leagues. Consistency zone.
  1.60-2.60    PRIME VALUE   40-60%      Main hunting ground. Margins are
               ZONE                      thinnest here, 3-6% edges are
                                         realistic, Kelly stakes meaningful.
                                         Most long-run PROFIT lives here
                                         despite the modest hit rate.
  2.60-4.00    SELECTIVE     26-38%      Good away teams vs overrated homes.
                                         Require edge >= 5-6%; variance is
                                         high; cap stakes at half normal.
  4.00-7.00    RARE          15-25%      Only with modeled edge >= 8% AND a
                                         structural reason confirmed (key
                                         absence, verified rotation,
                                         motivation mismatch). Quarter-Kelly
                                         keeps these stakes tiny anyway.
  > 7.00       NEVER            —        Favorite-longshot kill zone. Hard
                                         filter in the pick engine: never
                                         published, regardless of modeled
                                         edge — calibration is least
                                         trustworthy exactly here.

  DRAWS — SPECIAL CASE:
    Draw odds (typically 3.0-3.6) carry disproportionate margin, and
    models predict draws poorly (the draw is the residual outcome, and
    gradient-boosted 1X2 models systematically under-call it). Do NOT
    bet raw draws in MVP. Phase 6 specialist model only, on a narrow
    profile: evenly-Elo'd, low-xG, defensively strong pairs late in the
    season (France/Italy mid-table archetype). Until then, draw risk is
    handled by SELLING it (DNB, DC) rather than buying it.

4. BOOKMAKER LANDSCAPE & LINE SHOPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Classify every book in the bookmaker table (is_sharp flag exists in
  the schema; add a `limits_winners` boolean):

  SHARP (price-setters — use as TRUTH SIGNAL, bet for volume/longevity):
    * Pinnacle — lowest margins (2-3% on big leagues), welcomes winners,
      its closing line is the calibration benchmark for the whole system.
    * Betfair Exchange — no bookmaker margin (commission instead), true
      market prices, best for large stakes; liquidity thin in small
      leagues before matchday.
  SOFT (price-followers — where most VALUE is captured, until limited):
    * bet365, William Hill, Betway, Unibet/Kindred, 888, local books.
    * They lag sharp moves and run promotions => stale prices = your EV.
    * They limit winners; expect a soft-book account to have a lifetime.
  BEST-ODDS ENGINE RULES:
    * For every (match, market, selection): best price across books,
      minus a realistic availability haircut (1-2%) in backtests.
    * Line shopping alone is worth 1-3% ROI — the cheapest edge in the
      entire system. It is also the difference between a losing and a
      winning system at the margins.
    * Consensus probability = margin-weighted blend leaning on sharps;
      soft-vs-sharp divergence > 4-5% is itself a pick trigger (steam
      chase) and a feature (master plan 5.5).

5. LEAGUE PREDICTABILITY INDEX (LPI) — CONCRETE SCORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Score each league quarterly, 0-100. Build as a Django admin report.

    LPI = 25 * imbalance      (normalized Elo spread / rating Gini —
                               dominant top teams => certainty at the top)
        + 15 * (1 - draw_rate_norm)   (draws are the model-killer)
        + 15 * style_stability (season-over-season correlation of league
                               O2.5 & BTTS base rates; stable => derived
                               markets calibrate)
        + 10 * (1 - churn)     (managerial + roster mid-season turnover)
        + 15 * data_depth      (0.3 results-only, 0.7 + odds archive,
                               1.0 + xG/lineups/injuries)
        + 10 * market_softness (inverse of league fame/liquidity — EPL
                               ~0.05, Norwegian 2nd tier ~0.9)
        + 10 * integrity       (1.0 clean history; 0 on any fixing flags;
                               a single confirmed fixed match = league
                               suspended from the pick engine)

  Cutoffs: LPI >= 60 => eligible for pick generation
           45-59      => model-training data only
           < 45       => ingest-and-watch, no bets

  THE TRADE-OFF TRIANGLE (you rarely get all three):
      Predictability  x  Market Softness  x  Data Quality
   * Big-5 leagues: best data, decent predictability, HARDEST markets.
   * Mid-tier European leagues: good data, good predictability, medium
     softness  <-- the sweet spot for the VALUE tier.
   * Exotic/lower divisions: soft markets but data too thin — a modeled
     "edge" there is usually noise. Enter only with a proven data feed.

6. TIER 1 — BEST LEAGUES TO START WITH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  All have historical results + closing odds on football-data.co.uk
  (code in [brackets] = its CSV file code; "extra" = the extra-leagues
  file) and live coverage on API-Football. Base rates shown as
  (Home / Draw / Away | Over 2.5) — multi-season approximate ranges.

  6.1 DOMINANT-TEAM LEAGUES (SAFE-tier goldmines)
   * SCOTLAND Premiership [SC0]       (~40/24/36 | ~50-53%)
       Celtic/Rangers vs bottom-half = Europe's most certain fixtures.
       Away% is inflated BY the Old Firm winning away — model the split.
       Raw odds on them are tiny; bet the derived markets (DC/DNB/AH).
   * PORTUGAL Primeira Liga [P1]      (~43/25/32 | ~47-51%)
       Big-3 dominance (Benfica/Porto/Sporting); top-vs-bottom highly
       predictable; market meaningfully softer than Big-5.
   * NETHERLANDS Eredivisie [N1]      (~46/23/31 | ~56-62%)
       Twin edge: top-3 dominance AND Europe's most reliable Overs
       league. Over 1.5/2.5 models calibrate beautifully here.
   * AUSTRIA Bundesliga [extra]       (~44/24/32 | ~55-58%)
       Salzburg-era dominance, high scoring, softer market. Note the
       points-halving playoff split — the model must handle the format.
   * SWITZERLAND Super League [extra] (~43/25/32 | ~55-58%)
       Similar profile; YB/Basel dominance cycles.
   * GREECE Super League [G1]         (~43/26/31 | ~42-46%)
       Big-4 dominance, strong home advantage, LOW scoring => Unders +
       home DC. Integrity: monitor — historical fixing incidents.
   * TURKEY Super Lig [T1]            (~45/24/31 | ~51-55%)
       Istanbul-club dominance + one of Europe's strongest home edges.
       Volatile officiating => stick to 1X2/OU/DC, skip cards markets.

  6.2 BIG-5 LEAGUES (training mass + market-aware features; smaller edges)
   * GERMANY Bundesliga [D1]          (~44/24/32 | ~53-58%)
       Highest-scoring Big-5; Bayern-era top-end certainty; elite data.
       Best Big-5 league for Overs/BTTS models.
   * SPAIN La Liga [SP1]              (~46/25/29 | ~44-48%)
       Top-3 certainty; low scoring => Unders lean; strong home edge.
   * ENGLAND Premier League [E0]      (~44/24/32 | ~51-55%)
       Sharpest soccer market on earth. Use as CALIBRATION BENCHMARK
       and rich training data, not a primary profit source. If the
       model shows big EPL edges, suspect a bug before genius.
   * ITALY Serie A [I1]               (~41/27/32 | ~50-54%)
       Higher draw rate => harder 1X2; decent O/U + BTTS stability;
       late-season motivation asymmetries are genuinely exploitable.
   * FRANCE Ligue 1 [F1]              (~44/26/30 | ~48-52%)
       PSG-era top certainty; the rest of the table is noisy; draws
       frequent. Treat mid-table 1X2 with suspicion.

  6.3 HIGH-SCORING SPECIALISTS (build Overs/BTTS models here)
   * NORWAY Eliteserien [extra]       (O2.5 ~55-60%) — summer league:
       fills June-August when Europe sleeps.
   * SWEDEN Allsvenskan [extra]       (O2.5 ~53-57%) — same role.
   * GERMANY 2. Bundesliga [D2]       (O2.5 ~53-58%) — good data, softer
       market than the Bundesliga.
   * NETHERLANDS Eerste Divisie [N2-not on FDCO; API only]
       (O2.5 ~58-63%) — one of Europe's highest-scoring second tiers;
       thinner data => derived goals markets only, half stakes.

  6.4 LOW-SCORING / UNDERS SPECIALISTS
   * FRANCE Ligue 2 [F2]              (O2.5 ~40-45%) — chronic Unders.
   * ITALY Serie B [I2]               (low scoring, high draws) —
       Under 2.5 + DC value; avoid raw 1X2.
   * SPAIN LaLiga 2 [SP2]             (similar Unders + draws profile).

8. HARD EXCLUSIONS (FILTERS CODED INTO THE PICK ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * International friendlies       — motivation unknowable. Never.
   * Club friendlies / pre-season   — rotation lottery. Never.
   * Youth / reserve / U21 leagues  — data garbage + integrity risk.
   * Early cup rounds with rotation — unless the lineup-confirmed
     re-prediction (master plan 5.4) explicitly clears the pick.
   * Dead rubbers at season end     — excluded UNLESS the model carries
     an explicit motivation feature; then relegation six-pointers vs
     nothing-to-play-for sides become a niche edge, not a trap.
   * Any league with < 3 seasons of ingested history + odds archive.
   * Integrity auto-blacklist: sudden heavy odds moves on meaningless
     matches => flag the team pair, suspend picks, review manually.

10. PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION ACROSS LEAGUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * Cap any single league at 15% of daily staked volume (mirrors master
     plan 8.3) — league-level model failure is correlated failure.
   * Cap any single MATCH at 4% of bankroll across all its markets
     (same-match picks are one exposure, not several).
   * Specialize models per league-market pair: an Eredivisie-Overs model
     and a SerieB-Unders model will each beat one global everything-
     model. League embeddings in the pooled model cover the long tail.
   * Quarterly LPI review catches regime change (post-dominance Scotland
     or Austria seasons look very different) BEFORE the P&L does.

11. WORKED EXAMPLE — EV + QUARTER-KELLY, END TO END
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fixture: Feyenoord (H) vs bottom-3 side, Eredivisie.
  Model (calibrated ensemble):  P(home) = 0.62
  Best odds found (soft book):  2.00    (implied raw = 0.500)
  Market de-vig (Shin, all books): p_market = 0.575

    edge = 0.62 - 0.575          = +4.5%   (>= 3.0% threshold: PASS)
    odds window 1.45-2.20                   PASS
    EV   = 0.62 * (2.00-1) - 0.38 = +0.24  (+24% per unit staked)

    Full Kelly fraction f* = (p*odds - 1)/(odds - 1)
                           = (0.62*2.00 - 1)/(2.00 - 1) = 0.24
    Quarter-Kelly          = 0.06 of bankroll -> capped at 0.02 (2% cap)
    STAKE = 2% of bankroll.

  Sanity rails that must ALL pass before publishing:
    * p_model inside the model's proven calibration range   PASS
    * data completeness score for the match >= threshold    PASS
    * league LPI >= 60                                      PASS
    * not on integrity blacklist, not a dead rubber         PASS
  Published as VALUE pick; odds re-checked at publish; CLV recorded at
  kickoff against the closing line.
