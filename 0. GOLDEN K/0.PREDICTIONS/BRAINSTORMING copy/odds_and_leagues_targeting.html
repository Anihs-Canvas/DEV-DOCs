How to Use This File

🌱1 · Seed

Load the thresholds in §2, §3 and §12 into valuebets.RiskProfile and a new valuebets.MarketRule table — odds window, min edge, tier, and league whitelist per market.

🧪2 · Backtest

Run the walk-forward backtester over each (league, market, odds band) cell. Keep cells with positive yield and positive CLV over ≥ 2 seasons; disable the rest. Expect roughly half the cells to survive — that is normal and good.

🔄3 · Review

Re-run the LPI + cell review quarterly. Rules live in the DB and are edited in Django admin — never hard-coded.

01

Core Principles — Why Odds Bands Are Not Equally Beatable

🎯A · Favorite–Longshot Bias

The most documented inefficiency in sports betting: recreational money chases big payouts, so bookmakers shade longshot odds well below fair value and can price favorites close to fair.

  • Short-priced favorites lose the least to the margin.
  • Longshots (> ~7.0) are structurally the worst bets in soccer — expected loss on random longshots is several times worse than on random favorites.
  • Model calibration is weakest in the tails — a "big edge" on a longshot is more likely a model error than a market error.

🧮B · Margin Concentration

The bookmaker's vig is not spread evenly across a market's selections — disproportionate margin sits on the draw and the outsider. De-vigging with Shin's method (master plan §5.6) exposes this. Your value engine will naturally surface more edges on favorites and mid-range prices. Trust that pattern.

⏱️C · Timing — When to Take the Price (CLV Discipline)

  • Odds open ~5–7 days out at low limits, sharpen continuously, and are most accurate at kickoff (the closing line).
  • Soft books lag sharp moves by minutes-to-hours. The cheapest edge: detect a sharp-book move (Pinnacle / exchange) and take the stale price at a lagging soft book before it follows.
  • Model-liked favorites are usually best taken early (public money later shortens them); popular overs / big-club prices drift the other way. Backtest "price at T-24h vs closing" per market to find the optimal capture window empirically.
  • Track CLV on every bet (bet.clv). Consistently beating the close ⇒ profit follows. Negative CLV ⇒ stop and fix the model — don't wait for the bankroll to prove it.

🏦D · Liquidity & Limits Reality

Soft books limit or close winning accounts, often within weeks. Plan for it: spread volume across books, prefer exchanges (Betfair) and limit-friendly sharps (Pinnacle) as volume grows, and treat account longevity as a managed resource. This drives where the best-odds engine routes (§4).

> 7.00NEVER— Favorite–longshot kill zone. Hard filter in the pick engine: never published regardless of modeled edge — calibration is least trustworthy exactly here.

🤝Draws — Special Case

Draw odds (typically 3.0–3.6) carry disproportionate margin, and models predict draws poorly — the draw is the residual outcome, and gradient-boosted 1X2 models systematically under-call it. Do not bet raw draws in MVP. Phase 6 specialist model only, on a narrow profile: evenly-Elo'd, low-xG, defensively strong pairs late in the season (France/Italy mid-table archetype). Until then, draw risk is handled by selling it (DNB, DC) rather than buying it.

Bookmaker Landscape & Line Shopping

Classify every book in the bookmaker table (is_sharp flag exists in the schema; add a limits_winners boolean).

🦈SHARP — price-setters

Use as truth signal; bet for volume & longevity.

  • Pinnacle — lowest margins (2–3% on big leagues), welcomes winners; its closing line is the calibration benchmark for the whole system.
  • Betfair Exchange — no bookmaker margin (commission instead), true market prices, best for large stakes; liquidity thin in small leagues before matchday.

🧸SOFT — price-followers

Where most value is captured… until you get limited.

  • bet365, William Hill, Betway, Unibet/Kindred, 888, local books.
  • They lag sharp moves and run promotions ⇒ stale prices = your EV.
  • They limit winners; expect a soft-book account to have a lifetime.

🛒Best-Odds Engine Rules

  • For every (match, market, selection): best price across books, minus a realistic availability haircut (1–2%) in backtests.
  • Line shopping alone is worth 1–3% ROI — the cheapest edge in the entire system, and the difference between a losing and a winning system at the margins.
  • Consensus probability = margin-weighted blend leaning on sharps; soft-vs-sharp divergence > 4–5% is itself a pick trigger (steam chase) and a model feature (master plan §5.5).

Hard Exclusions

Filters coded into the pick engine — not guidelines.

  • International friendlies — motivation unknowable. Never.
  • Club friendlies / pre-season — rotation lottery. Never.
  • Youth / reserve / U21 leagues — data garbage + integrity risk.
  • Early cup rounds with rotation — unless the lineup-confirmed re-prediction (master plan §5.4) explicitly clears the pick.
  • Dead rubbers at season end — excluded unless the model carries an explicit motivation feature; then relegation six-pointers vs nothing-to-play-for sides become a niche edge, not a trap.
  • Any league with < 3 seasons of ingested history + odds archive.
  • Integrity auto-blacklist — sudden heavy odds moves on meaningless matches ⇒ flag the team pair, suspend picks, review manually.

Portfolio Construction Across Leagues

🧯League cap — 15%

Cap any single league at 15% of daily staked volume (mirrors master plan §8.3) — league-level model failure is correlated failure.

🎫Match cap — 4%

Cap any single match at 4% of bankroll across all its markets — same-match picks are one exposure, not several.

🧠Specialize per league-market pair

An Eredivisie-Overs model and a Serie B-Unders model will each beat one global everything-model. League embeddings in the pooled model cover the long tail.

📉Quarterly LPI review

Catches regime change (post-dominance Scotland or Austria seasons look very different) before the P&L does.

Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet

🛡️Safest Combos SAFE · 85%+ hit-rate targets

  • Eredivisie / Bundesliga / Austria: Over 1.5 goals @ 1.15–1.35
  • Scotland / Portugal / Greece top clubs vs bottom-4:
    Double Chance 1X @ 1.10–1.25 · Draw No Bet @ 1.20–1.45
  • Big favorites anywhere: Asian Handicap −1.0 @ 1.35–1.55 — push insurance = safest way to back a heavy favorite.

💰Best Profit Hunting VALUE · 1.60–2.60

  • Mid-tier European 1X2 favorites the market slightly underrates: Portugal, Netherlands, Austria, Denmark, Turkish home sides.
  • O/U 2.5 in style-stable leagues: NED/GER/NOR overs; F2/I2 unders.
  • BTTS in Eredivisie / Bundesliga / Eliteserien.
  • Steam-lag captures: sharp book moves, soft book hasn't followed.

⏰Timing

  • Model-liked favorites: take prices early (T-3d to T-24h).
  • Everything else: empirical per-market capture window from backtest.
  • Every bet's CLV recorded; negative rolling CLV = stop and fix.

🚫Never

  • Odds > 7.0 · raw draws (until the Phase 6 specialist) · friendlies · youth football.
  • Correct score / HT-FT · leagues with < 3 seasons of data.
  • Any pick without a de-vigged edge above its market threshold.
  • Lowering thresholds to fill a thin day.
