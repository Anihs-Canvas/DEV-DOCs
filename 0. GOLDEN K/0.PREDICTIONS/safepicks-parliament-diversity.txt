isolates whether two models deviate from the line in the SAME direction.

================================================================================
4. VERDICT
================================================================================
* EFFECTIVE distinct engines for the core pick = TWO (Dixon-Coles; LightGBM
  stack). Not five. A "5 models voting" framing on 1X2 is THEATER: stack/blend/
  dc-v2-xg-w0 copy or clone dc-v1 (prob corr 1.000, 0.999, 1.000).
* On DC / DNB / OU markets there IS genuine — but modest — diversity: the two
  families' deviations from the market correlate only ~0.5, and the GBM stack
  disagrees with the DC call ~4% of the time and independently vetoes ~10% of the
  mainline's OU1.5 mistakes. That is real information, not zero.
* BEST genuinely-diverse roster (2-3 members):
    1. dc-v1        (Dixon-Coles, active mainline)
    2. stack-v1     (LightGBM stack; or stack-v3, stacked on dc-v2) -- the ONLY
                     cross-family voter available (residual corr ~0.5 with dc-v1)
    3. b0-market    (the market line) -- OPTIONAL 3rd veto: genuinely different
                     (prob corr ~0.81 vs DC, ~0.88 vs stack) and the single best
                     predictor; "does the close agree?" is the most valuable safe
                     veto, though it is the benchmark, not a model.
* A 4th/5th member would be PADDING: dc-v2 / dc-v2-xg are 0.95-1.0 correlated
  with dc-v1 (same engine; dc-v2-xg is pinned at w=0 == dc-v1); stack-v2 / blend-v1
  are 0.92-0.97 correlated with stack-v1. Adding them inflates the vote count
  without adding an independent opinion. Justifying a real 3rd/4th member needs a
  NEW engine — e.g. a truly independent xG/shot-based model that is not just dc-v1
  blended, or per-league market-microstructure probabilities distinct from the
  devig baseline. Specialist models (cards/corners/halves/etc.) do not qualify:
  they vote on other markets, not the same pick.

BOTTOM LINE: The parliament is diverse enough to be worth ~2 (at most 3) seats,
not 5. A K-of-N gate across dc-v1 + a GBM-stack (+ optionally the market line)
adds genuine robustness (catches ~10% of single-model mistakes on OU/DC). A gate
that counts dc-v1, dc-v2, dc-v2-xg, stack-v1, stack-v2 as five independent voters
is largely theater — three of those five are near-duplicates of the other two.
