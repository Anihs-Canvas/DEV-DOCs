☸️ Kubernetes CKA — Certified Kubernetes Administrator

The Complete, Self-Contained Mastery Guide

From total beginner to CKA-certified administrator — and beyond. Every concept is built on the easypj Django project (jobpost app, PostgreSQL), aligned to the official 2025 CKA syllabus and packed with diagrams, labs, and real-world Day-2 operations.

🟢 Beginner-Friendly ☸️ Kubernetes v1.31+ 🐍 Django · easypj / jobpost 📚 14 Parts · 45 Chapters 🎯 2025 Syllabus · 100% Coverage 📋 12 Appendices

👋 Welcome. This is a complete, self-contained path from "what is a container?" to CKA-certified administrator — and the real-world operations beyond the exam. You never need another doc, blog, book, or video.

Everything is taught on one real app, easypj (a Django jobpost project on PostgreSQL): you'll containerize it, deploy it, scale it, secure it, break it, fix it, and ship it. Aligned 100% to the official 2025 CKA syllabus. Use the ☰ sidebar to jump anywhere.
