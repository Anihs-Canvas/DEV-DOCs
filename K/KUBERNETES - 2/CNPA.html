🏗️ Cloud Native Platform Engineering

CNPA — Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate

A comprehensive study guide covering all 6 official CNPA exam domains. Built with anihpj (Django) & jobpost app as the hands-on learning vehicle.

🟢 Beginner-Friendly☸️ Kubernetes🏗️ Platform Eng🔄 CI/CD🔐 Security📊 Observability📋 28 Chapters

Cloud Native Platform Engineering is the practice of designing, building, and maintaining Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) that enable development teams to ship software faster, safer, and more reliably on cloud-native infrastructure.
Think of it as "building the paved road" so developers don't have to navigate the muddy path of Kubernetes, CI/CD, monitoring, and infrastructure themselves.
