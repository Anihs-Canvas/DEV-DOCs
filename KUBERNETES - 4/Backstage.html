⛵ Backstage CBA — Complete Learning Guide

Spotify's Internal Developer Portal — From Zero to CBA Certified

A comprehensive, highly-illustrated guide covering EVERYTHING you need to know to pass the Certified Backstage Associate (CBA) exam. Using the anihpj Django project with jobpost app as a real-world example.

🎯 CBA Exam Focused 📚 17 Chapters 🔧 20+ Diagrams 🎯 170+ Practice Questions ⚓ Backstage v1.x 🌟 CNCF Incubating 📦 anihpj/jobpost
